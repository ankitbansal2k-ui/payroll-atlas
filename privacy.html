Privacy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Payroll Atlas is a read-only reference site. It does not ask you for personal information.

What we collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, forms or newsletters.
  • No cookies and no tracking or analytics scripts.
  • No advertising and no third-party embeds. Fonts, scripts and map data are served from this site itself, so your browser makes no requests to other companies when you load a page.

Your browser, on your device

Choosing a country or filter is handled entirely in your browser. The choice may appear in the page address (for example ?country=uk) so you can share a link. It is not sent anywhere else or stored.

Hosting logs

The site is served by a hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages, and possibly Vercel or a similar service). Like any web host, the provider may keep standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We do not control or receive those logs. See the provider's own privacy statement for details.

Links to other sites

Each entry links to the official or professional page it is based on. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

Reporting an error or contacting us

Use GitHub Issues. Anything you post there is public and is subject to GitHub's terms and privacy statement, so please do not include personal or confidential information.

Changes

If this changes (for example if analytics is added), this page will be updated before the change goes live.

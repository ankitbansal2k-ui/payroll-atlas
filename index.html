FAQ

Common questions

Is Intelligent Payroll really free?

Yes. There's no account, no paywall, and no trial period. Every country page and the full changelog are open to anyone.

How often is the data updated?

Every two weeks. We check official and professional tax sources for new or amended statutory and legislative changes, and publish an update every other Sunday evening, Central European Time.

Where does the information come from?

Government authorities where we can retrieve them, and major tax advisory publications where we cannot. Every entry shows which. We don't rely on rumours or unsourced summaries.

Which countries are covered?

75 countries today, across Europe (30), APAC (15), MENAT (13), LATAM (14), and Africa (3). Use the country selector in the header to see the full list.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. Intelligent Payroll is a reference tool to help you track when and where changes happen. Always confirm details with a qualified advisor before acting on them.

I found an error or an outdated entry. How do I report it?

Open an issue on our GitHub repository, linked in the footer. We review and correct entries as part of the fortnightly update.

Is coverage complete for every country?

No. Coverage is partial and grows with every update. Some countries have only one or two entries, and an absence of entries does not mean nothing has changed. We add only changes we can tie to a source page, so we would rather show less than guess.

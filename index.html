FAQ

Common questions

Is Payroll Atlas really free?

Yes. There's no account, no paywall, and no trial period. Every country page and the full changelog are open to anyone.

How often is the data updated?

We run a compliance sweep monthly, checking official and professional tax sources for new or amended statutory and legislative changes.

Where does the information come from?

Directly from government tax authorities, labour inspectorates, and social security bodies in each country. We don't rely on secondary sources or aggregators.

Which countries are covered?

70 countries today, across Europe (29), APAC (15), MENAT (12), and LATAM (14). Use the country selector in the header to see the full list.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. Payroll Atlas is a reference tool to help you track when and where changes happen. Always confirm details with a qualified advisor before acting on them.

I found an error or an outdated entry. How do I report it?

Open an issue on our GitHub repository, linked in the footer. We review and correct entries as part of the monthly sweep.

Do you plan to expand beyond Europe?

Not currently. Scope is Europe only for now, so we can keep every entry accurate rather than spreading coverage thin.

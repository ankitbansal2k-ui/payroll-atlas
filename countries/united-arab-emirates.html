AED 6,000 minimum monthly wage for Emiratis in the private sector

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

MoHRE requires a minimum monthly wage of AED 6,000 for Emiratis employed in the private sector from 1 January 2026, with salaries of existing Emirati staff to be adjusted by 30 June 2026.

For the employer

  • Apply the AED 6,000 monthly wage to Emirati work permits issued, renewed or amended from 1 January 2026.
  • Adjust salaries of Emiratis hired before 2026 to at least AED 6,000 by 30 June 2026.
  • From 1 July 2026, underpaid Emiratis are excluded from Emiratisation quota counts and new work permits may be suspended.

For the employee

  • Emirati private-sector employees are entitled to a minimum monthly wage of AED 6,000.

Emiratisation H1 2026 target and AED 10,000 monthly contribution

Status: In force 30 Jun 2026 Deadline 30 June 2026

Private-sector companies with 50 or more employees had to add 1% Emiratis in skilled roles by 30 June 2026 (2% annual target); financial contributions apply from 1 July 2026.

For the employer

  • Financial contribution from 1 July 2026: AED 10,000 per month (AED 120,000 per year) for each unfilled Emirati position.
  • Companies with outstanding Emiratisation results can join the Emiratisation Partners Club (discounts of up to 80% on MoHRE service fees).

In practice: Two unfilled positions: 2 x AED 120,000 = AED 240,000 per year.

MoHRE directs employers to the Nafis platform for recruiting Emirati jobseekers.

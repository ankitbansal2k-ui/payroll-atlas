🇹🇼 Taiwan: payroll law changes

Minimum wage rises to NT$29,500 a month; hourly rate to NT$196

Effective 1 Jan 2026

Taiwan's Ministry of Labor raises the monthly minimum wage from NT$28,590 to NT$29,500 and the hourly rate from NT$190 to NT$196 (both a 3.18% adjustment), effective 1 January 2026 - the 10th consecutive year of monthly minimum wage increases since 2016.

For the employer

  • Raise any employee paid below NT$29,500/month or NT$196/hour to at least the new minimum from 1 January 2026.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage employees receive a NT$910 monthly (NT$6 hourly) increase from January 2026.

In practice: NT$29,500 - NT$28,590 = NT$910 per month.

Source: Ministry of Labor, Taiwan - 2026 Minimum Wage Announcement · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 29 September 2026

Standard deduction, exemption, and salary special deduction thresholds raised for 2026

Effective 2026 tax year

Taiwan raises several individual income tax thresholds for the 2026 tax year to reflect cost-of-living changes: the standard deduction rises NT$5,000 to NT$136,000 for single filers (NT$10,000 to NT$272,000 for married joint filers), the personal exemption rises NT$4,000 to NT$101,000 (NT$151,500 for those 70+), and the special deduction for wage/salary earners and persons with disabilities rises from NT$218,000 to NT$227,000.

For the employer

  • Update payroll withholding tax tables to the 2026 exemption and deduction thresholds.

For the employee

  • Slightly less income tax withheld in 2026 due to the higher exemption and deduction thresholds, even without a pay change.

Source: Overseas Community Affairs Council, Taiwan - 2026 Tax Threshold Adjustments · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 29 September 2026

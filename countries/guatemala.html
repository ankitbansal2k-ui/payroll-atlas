New minimum wages by sector take effect under Acuerdo Gubernativo 256-2025

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

Guatemala's Ministry of Labour and Social Welfare sets new minimum wages by economic activity and jurisdiction under Acuerdo Gubernativo 256-2025, effective 1 January 2026. In Guatemala department (C1): non-agricultural Q131.58/day (Q4,002.28/month). Rest of country (C2): non-agricultural Q125.49/day.

For the employer

  • Update payroll to the applicable 2026 minimum wage by economic activity (agricultural, non-agricultural, export/maquila) and jurisdiction (C1 Guatemala department vs. C2 rest of country) from 1 January 2026.

For the employee

  • Minimum wage varies by sector and region - check the applicable rate for your specific economic activity and jurisdiction.

Social security contribution rates: employer 12.67%, employee 4.83%

Status: Ongoing Current rates

Employers in Guatemala contribute 12.67% of monthly payroll and employees 4.83% of monthly salary toward social security.

For the employer

  • Contribute 12.67% of monthly payroll.
  • Withhold 4.83% of each employee's monthly salary and remit it with the employer contribution.

For the employee

  • Social security deduction: 4.83% of monthly salary.

In practice: On a Q10,000 monthly salary: employer Q1,267 (12.67%); employee Q483 (4.83%).

🇸🇻 El Salvador: payroll law changes

Minimum wage rises 12% across all sectors

Status: In force 1 Jun 2025

El Salvador raised the minimum wage by 12% from 1 June 2025, with rates set by decree for agriculture, industry, commerce, services, and textile and apparel manufacturing.

For the employer

  • Apply the 12% higher minimum wage for the relevant sector from 1 June 2025.

For the employee

  • Minimum wage workers receive a 12% increase from 1 June 2025.

Source: Diario El Mundo - El salario mínimo por sector económico a partir del 1 de junio · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 30 September 2026

ISSS (social security) and AFP (pension) contribution rates

Status: Ongoing Current rates

ISSS social security contributions are 3% (employee) and 7.5% (employer) on salaries up to USD 1,000/month, capped at USD 30 and USD 75 respectively for higher salaries. AFP pension contributions are 7.25% (employee) and 8.75% (employer).

For the employer

  • ISSS: 7.5% of salary, maximum USD 75/month per employee.
  • AFP: 8.75% of salary.
  • Withhold employee ISSS (3%, max USD 30) and AFP (7.25%) and remit them monthly.

For the employee

  • ISSS: 3% of salary, maximum USD 30/month.
  • AFP: 7.25% of salary.

In practice: On a USD 1,500 monthly salary: ISSS employee USD 30, employer USD 75; AFP employee USD 108.75, employer USD 131.25. Totals: employee USD 138.75, employer USD 206.25.

Source: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries - El Salvador, Individual - Other taxes · Payroll impact: Low: little or no payroll configuration needed · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 29 September 2026

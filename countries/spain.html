Salario Minimo Interprofesional (SMI)

€40.70 per day or €1,221 per month (depending on whether pay is set by day or by month), annual figure €17,094, with effect from 1 January 2026

Valid from 1 January 2026

Real Decreto 126/2026 of 18 Feb 2026 (BOE no. 44, 19 Feb 2026): +3.1% on 2025, effect from 1 Jan 2026. Art. 3.1 states an annual figure never below €17,094. The decree does not state a number of annual payments.

Source: Boletin Oficial del Estado - Real Decreto 126/2026, por el que se fija el salario minimo interprofesional para 2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employer social security contribution rates (Regimen General)

Common contingencies 23.60%; unemployment 5.50% (indefinite contracts and listed types) or 6.70% (fixed-term); FOGASA 0.20%; vocational training 0.60%; MEI (intergenerational equity mechanism) 0.75%; accident at work and occupational illness: per the premium tariff, employer only

Valid from 1 January 2026

Orden PJC/297/2026 (BOE 31 Mar 2026), in force 1 Apr 2026 with effect from 1 Jan 2026. Temporary-incapacity rate for workers past retirement age: 1.55% (1.30% employer, 0.25% employee). Fixed-term contracts under 30 days: extra employer payment of €33.62.

Source: Boletín Oficial del Estado - Orden PJC/297/2026 (cotización a la Seguridad Social, desempleo, FOGASA y formación profesional 2026) · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employee social security contribution rates (Regimen General)

Common contingencies 4.70%; unemployment 1.55% (indefinite contracts and listed types) or 1.60% (fixed-term contracts); vocational training 0.10%; MEI 0.15%

Valid from 1 January 2026

Overtime due to force majeure: employer 12.00%, employee 2.00% (total 14.00%); other overtime follows common-contingency rates (23.60% / 4.70%).

Source: Boletín Oficial del Estado - Orden PJC/297/2026 (cotización a la Seguridad Social, desempleo, FOGASA y formación profesional 2026) · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Maximum base €5,101.20 per month in every group; minimum monthly base: Group 1 €1,989.30, Group 2 €1,649.70, Group 3 €1,435.20, Groups 4 to 7 €1,424.40; accident-at-work contribution base: minimum €1,424.40, maximum €5,101.20 per month

Valid from 1 January 2026

Orden PJC/297/2026 arts. 2 and 3, from 1 Jan 2026. Groups 8 to 11 use daily bases (minimum €47.48, maximum €170.04 per day), not listed here.

Personal income tax (IRPF) withholding scale for employment income (art. 85)

Up to €12,450: 19%; €12,450 to €20,200: 24%; €20,200 to €35,200: 30%; €35,200 to €60,000: 37%; €60,000 to €300,000: 45%; over €300,000: 47%

Base máxima €5,101.20; MEI rate 0.90%

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

For 2026 the maximum contribution base (base máxima de cotización) is €5,101.20 a month. The Mecanismo de Equidad Intergeneracional (MEI) contribution from 1 January 2026 is 0.9% of the common-contingencies base, of which 0.75% is paid by the employer and 0.15% by the employee.

For the employer

  • Update payroll with the 2026 base máxima of €5,101.20 and the 0.75% employer MEI share.

For the employee

  • Employees pay 0.15% MEI on their common-contingencies base.

In practice: At a €5,101.20 monthly base, MEI is €45.91 (employer €38.26, employee €7.65).

Source: Seguridad Social - Bases y tipos de cotización 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

SMI rises 3.1% to €1,221 a month, payable from 1 January 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026 Effective 1 Jan 2026 (retroactive)

Royal Decree 126/2026 sets the Spanish minimum wage (SMI) for 2026 at €1,221 a month or €40.70 a day, 3.1% more than in 2025. In annual terms the SMI is never taken as less than €17,094. The decree came into force on 20 February 2026 and the SMI is payable with effect from 1 January 2026.

For the employer

  • Pay at least €1,221 a month (or €40.70 a day, if pay is set by the day) for the full legal working time, and pro rata for shorter hours, with effect from 1 January 2026.
  • Compare annual pay with the SMI plus the salary supplements named in article 2, never taking less than €17,094. Pay that falls below that annual total must be raised to it (article 3).
  • Workers hired for up to 120 days by the same employer: minimum €57.82 per working day. Domestic workers paid by the hour: €9.55 per hour worked.

For the employee

  • Workers paid by the month are entitled to at least €1,221, or at least €40.70 per day if pay is set by the day, with effect from 1 January 2026.
  • Only pay in money counts towards the SMI; pay in kind cannot reduce the amount due in money.

Source: BOE - Real Decreto 126/2026, por el que se fija el salario mínimo interprofesional para 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026 · Updated 4 October 2026

TGSS reverses itself: notice-in-lieu pay now subject to social security contributions

Status: In force 14 Jul 2026 Criterion change: 14 Jul 2026

The Tesorería General de la Seguridad Social (TGSS) reversed its own prior criterion: amounts paid to an employee for an employer's failure to give notice on an objective dismissal (Article 53 ET) must now be included in the social security contribution base, where they were previously excluded. Employers who paid notice-in-lieu under the old criterion need to file a complementary settlement using CRA code 0054.

For the employer

  • Include notice-in-lieu payments on objective dismissals in the contribution base going forward.
  • File a complementary settlement (CRA code 0054) for any notice-in-lieu payments made under the old, excluded criterion.

For the employee

  • Notice-in-lieu payments on an objective dismissal are now included in the social security contribution base.

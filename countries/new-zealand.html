🇳🇿 New Zealand: payroll law changes

2 tracked changes · APAC · sources last checked 1 October 2026

Minimum wage rises to NZD $23.95 an hour

Status: In force 1 Apr 2026

The adult minimum wage rises from NZD $23.50 to $23.95 an hour from 1 April 2026, a 45-cent increase. The starting-out and training minimum wage rises from $18.80 to $19.16 an hour.

For the employer

  • Raise any adult employee paid at or near $23.50/hour to at least $23.95 from the 1 April 2026 pay run.
  • Update starting-out and training wage rates to the new $19.16/hour floor.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage employees receive a 45-cent hourly pay rise from 1 April 2026.

KiwiSaver default contribution rate rises to 3.5%

Status: In force 1 Apr 2026

The default KiwiSaver (KiwiSaver - New Zealand's voluntary, work-based retirement savings scheme) employee and matching employer contribution rate rises from 3% to 3.5% from 1 April 2026, applying to all pay days from that date. Employees who want to keep contributing at 3% can apply to Inland Revenue for a temporary rate reduction, which employers can choose to match.

For the employer

  • Update payroll systems to deduct and match KiwiSaver contributions at the new 3.5% default rate from the first pay day on or after 1 April 2026.
  • Where an employee has an approved temporary rate reduction, you may choose to match their reduced rate instead of the 3.5% default.
  • From 1 April 2026, make employer contributions for 16- and 17-year-old employees who are KiwiSaver members, if they meet the other eligibility criteria.

For the employee

  • Default KiwiSaver contributions rise from 3% to 3.5% of pay (both employee and matching employer share) unless a different rate is chosen or a temporary reduction is approved.

Statutory minimum monthly salary (white-collar) and daily wage (blue-collar)

EUR 920.00 a month for white-collar workers and EUR 41.09 a day for blue-collar workers, from 1 April 2026 (Ministerial Decision 8934/27.3.2026, Gazette B 1759)

Valid from 1 April 2026

Seniority increments: white-collar 10% every three years up to 30% (nine years); blue-collar 5% every three years up to 30% (eighteen years). Contracts may not provide pay below the statutory minimum.

Source: Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs (Greece) - Minimum Wage · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employer social security contribution rate (e-EFKA), coverage package 101

21.79% of earnings (package code 101 "ΜΙΚΤΑ, ΙΚΑ-ΤΕΑΜ"), valid from 1 January 2025

Table of coverage packages (ΚΠΚ) in e-EFKA circular 38/2024: package 101 shows insured 13.37%, employer 21.79%, total 35.16%. Other packages differ (e.g. 102: employer 22.79%).

Maximum insurable monthly earnings (EFKA)

EUR 7,761.94 a month from 1 January 2026

Valid from 1 January 2026

Set by Ministry of Labour document Δ.15/Δ΄/1865/23-01-2026 (Gazette B 318/29-01-2026), per e-EFKA. The minimum monthly base for full-time employees is EUR 920.00 from 1 April 2026.

Source: e-EFKA - Γενικές ερωτήσεις (Μισθωτοί) · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Income tax scale on employment and pension income, tax years 2026 onwards (base scale)

9% on the first EUR 10,000; 20% on the next 10,000; 26% on the next 10,000; 34% on the next 10,000; 39% on 40,001-60,000; 44% above EUR 60,000

Base rates of the first column; the source lists lower rates by number of dependent children and for young people (up to 25: zero up to EUR 20,000; 26 to 30: 9% on the second band). Tax credits are not covered.

New 2026 income tax scale with lower middle rates

Status: In force 2026 Effective income year 2026

For a taxpayer over 30 without children, the 2026 scale is 9% up to €10,000, 20% from €10,000 to €20,000, 26% from €20,000 to €30,000, 34% from €30,000 to €40,000, 39% from €40,000 to €60,000, and 44% above €60,000. Separate scales apply by age and number of children.

For the employer

  • Load the new 2026 scales (including age- and child-based variants) into payroll withholding.

For the employee

  • Most employees see lower withholding compared with 2025.

Source: Taxheaven - Οι νέες αναλυτικές κλίμακες φορολογίας 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Minimum wage rises to €920

Status: In force 1 Apr 2026

From 1 April 2026 the minimum monthly wage rises from €880 to €920, the sixth consecutive increase. The government targets €950 by 2027.

For the employer

  • Raise pay for employees at the minimum and review seniority scales and allowances tied to the minimum wage.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage workers receive €40 more a month.

In practice: €920 - €880 = €40 a month increase.

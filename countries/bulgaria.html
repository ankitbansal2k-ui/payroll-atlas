🇧🇬 Bulgaria: payroll law changes

2 tracked changes · Europe · sources last checked 1 October 2026

Statutory and legislative payroll changes in Bulgaria, each linked to the page it was checked against. For information only, not legal or tax advice: confirm details with the source before acting. See the terms.

Minimum wage rises 12.6% to EUR 620.20

Effective 1 Jan 2026

The Council of Ministers set the minimum monthly wage at BGN 1,213 (EUR 620.20) from 1 January 2026, an increase of BGN 136 (12.6%). The minimum hourly wage is BGN 7.31 (EUR 3.74).

For the employer

  • Raise any employee paid below EUR 620.20 a month (EUR 3.74 an hour) from the January 2026 pay run, and re-check minimum-wage-linked allowances.

For the employee

  • Workers on the minimum wage get a 12.6% increase from January 2026.

Source: BTA - Cabinet Raises Minimum Wage by 12.6% to EUR 620.20 from January 1, 2026 · Last verified 1 October 2026

Maximum insurable income rises to EUR 2,300; civil servants pay own contributions

Effective 1 Aug 2026

Under the 2026 State Social Insurance Budget Act, the maximum insurable income rose to EUR 2,300 and the minimum insurable income for self-employed persons to EUR 620.20 from 1 August 2026. From the same date, civil servants under the Civil Servants Act and employees under the Judiciary Act pay personal social security contributions under an 80:20 employer-to-employee ratio, changing to 60:40 from 1 January 2027.

For the employer

  • Apply the EUR 2,300 maximum insurable income from the August 2026 payroll.
  • Public-sector employers: withhold the employee share for civil servants and judiciary staff (80:20 in 2026, 60:40 from 2027).

For the employee

  • Civil servants and judiciary staff see a personal social security deduction from August 2026.

Source: BTA - Social Security Income Thresholds Increase from August 1 · Last verified 1 October 2026

Withholding tax bands raised for 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

For 2026, the pay-as-you-earn withholding (staðgreiðsla (Pay-as-you-earn withholding)) rates published by Skatturinn (the Icelandic tax authority) are 31.49% on monthly income up to 498,122 kr., 37.99% from 498,123 to 1,398,450 kr. and 46.29% above that. In 2025 the first two limits were 472,005 kr. and 1,325,127 kr.; the rates are unchanged. Each rate is the income tax rate for the band plus the average municipal tax (útsvar (Municipal income tax)) of 14.94%.

For the employer

  • Load the 2026 band limits (498,122 kr. and 1,398,450 kr. a month) into payroll and calculate withholding on each month's pay separately.
  • Employees with pay from more than one employer must tell each employer which band to use; the tax credit is then deducted from the calculated withholding.

For the employee

  • Employees with pay from more than one employer must tell each employer which band applies, so the right share is withheld and no tax debt arises at assessment.

In practice: Illustration (ignores the pension deduction): monthly pay of 600,000 kr. in 2026: 498,122 x 31.49% + (600,000 - 498,122) x 37.99% = about 195,562 kr. before the personal tax credit of 72,492 kr., leaving about 123,070 kr. withheld. The first band limit rose by 26,117 kr. (498,122 - 472,005).

The 2025 limits (472,005 and 1,325,127 kr.) are from Skatturinn's 2025 page https://www.skatturinn.is/einstaklingar/stadgreidsla/skattthrep/2025/skattthrep-i-stadgreidslu-2025. The 2026 band page still contains an example that mentions a first-band limit of 472,005 kr. (the 2025 figure); its table and the staðgreiðsla 2026 page both say 498,122 kr.

Source: Skatturinn - Skattþrep í staðgreiðslu 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 12 October 2026

Personal tax credit rises to 72,492 kr. a month in 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The personal tax credit (persónuafsláttur (Personal tax credit)) is 869,898 kr. a year, or 72,492 kr. a month, in 2026, up from 824,288 kr. a year (68,691 kr. a month) in 2025. It is deducted from the calculated withholding. Unused credit builds up during the year but does not carry over to the next year.

For the employer

  • Update the monthly credit to 72,492 kr. (half month 36,246 kr., fourteen days 33,366 kr., one week 16,683 kr.); for other pay periods use 869,898 / 365 x days in the period.
  • Apply the credit only as the employee instructs: whether to use it, from which month, any accumulated credit and which band.

For the employee

  • Tell the employer whether to use the credit, from which month, any accumulated credit and which band; with several employers, say what share to use at each.
  • Using more credit than allowed can cause a tax debt at assessment.

In practice: Monthly credit rise: 72,492 - 68,691 = 3,801 kr.; annual rise: 869,898 - 824,288 = 45,610 kr. For a 14-day pay period: 869,898 / 365 x 14 = about 33,366 kr.

The 2025 figures (824,288 kr. a year, 68,691 kr. a month) are from Skatturinn's 2025 page https://www.skatturinn.is/einstaklingar/stadgreidsla/stadgreidsla/2025/stadgreidsla-2025. Skatturinn's personal tax credit page https://www.skatturinn.is/einstaklingar/stadgreidsla/personuafslattur/ says unused credit does not carry over between years and that using more credit than allowed can cause a tax debt at assessment. Up to 100% of a spouse's unused credit may be used in 2026.

Source: Skatturinn - Staðgreiðsla 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 12 October 2026

Employer social security levy (tryggingagjald) stays at 6.35% for 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

For 2026 the employer levy tryggingagjald (Social security levy (employer payroll charge)) is 6.35% of total pay, the same as in 2022 to 2025. It consists of the general levy 4.90%, the employment levy 1.35%, the Wage Guarantee Fund charge 0.05% and the market charge 0.05%. A further 0.65% applies to the pay of seamen on fishing vessels.

For the employer

  • Keep the 6.35% rate for 2026 (4.90% + 1.35% + 0.05% + 0.05%) and add 0.65% for seamen on fishing vessels (7.00% in total).
  • Report and pay the levy monthly together with the withholding; the due date is the 1st of the month after the pay month.

For the employee

  • No direct employee deduction; the levy is paid by the employer.

In practice: On total pay of 1,000,000 kr. in a month, the levy is 1,000,000 x 6.35% = 63,500 kr. (49,000 + 13,500 + 500 + 500).

For workers from EEA states holding an A1 certificate the source gives a reduced rate of 0.293% for 2025 (assessment 2026); the page states no 2026 figure for this group.

Source: Skatturinn - Tryggingagjald · Payroll impact: Low: little or no payroll configuration needed · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 12 October 2026

Children's tax-free allowance raised to 300,000 kr. in 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

In 2026 children, meaning those born in 2011 or later, are taxed at 6% (4% income tax and 2% municipal tax) on income above a children's allowance of 300,000 kr. In 2025 (children born 2010 or later) the allowance was 180,000 kr.

For the employer

  • Check with Skatturinn how the 300,000 kr. allowance is applied in withholding (the page does not say per month or per year) before configuring payroll.

For the employee

  • Young workers born in 2011 or later pay 6% on income above a children's allowance of 300,000 kr. in 2026.

In practice: The allowance rose by 300,000 - 180,000 = 120,000 kr. compared with 2025.

The 2025 allowance of 180,000 kr. (children born 2010 or later) is from Skatturinn's 2025 page https://www.skatturinn.is/einstaklingar/stadgreidsla/stadgreidsla/2025/stadgreidsla-2025. The 2026 source page does not say whether the allowance is per month or per year; confirm with Skatturinn before configuring payroll.

🇸🇰 Slovakia: payroll law changes

3 tracked changes · Europe · sources last checked 1 October 2026

Income tax moves to four bands: 19%, 25%, 30% and 35%

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

From 1 January 2026, the progressive personal income tax on employment income uses four bands: 19% up to €43,983.32, 25% from €43,983.33 to €60,349.21, 30% from €60,349.22 to €75,010.32, and 35% above €75,010.32.

For the employer

  • Confirm payroll software has loaded the new four-band structure before the first January pay run.

For the employee

  • Employees with annual income above €43,983.32 face marginal rates of 25%, 30% or 35% on the portion above each threshold.

Source: Podnikajte.sk - Progresívne zdanenie príjmov fyzických osôb od roku 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Employee health insurance rate rises from 4% to 5%

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

From January 2026 the employee health insurance contribution rises by 1 percentage point, from 4% to 5% of gross pay (from 2% to 2.5% for employees with disabilities). The employer health insurance rate stays at 11%. Total employee contributions reach 14.4% of gross pay, while total employer contributions are 36.2%.

For the employer

  • Update payroll with the 5% employee health insurance rate (2.5% for employees with disabilities); the employer rate is unchanged at 11%.

For the employee

  • Take-home pay falls slightly at an unchanged salary, since the employee health insurance rate rises from 4% to 5%.

Source: Kryptomagazín - Odvody zamestnanca a zamestnávateľa v roku 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Minimum wage rises €99 to €915

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The minimum wage is €915 a month from 1 January 2026, €99 more than in 2025 - a historic increase. It is set automatically at 60% of the average wage, and night and weekend work surcharges rise with it.

For the employer

  • Raise any employee paid below €915/month from the January 2026 pay run, and re-check minimum wage-linked surcharges for night and weekend work.

For the employee

  • Workers on the minimum wage get the largest automatic increase in the wage's history from January 2026.

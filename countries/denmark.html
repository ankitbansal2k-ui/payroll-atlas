Minimum wage (no statutory national minimum)

Denmark does not have a statutory minimum wage; pay is regulated by collective agreements or negotiated individually

Written by the Ministry of Employment, updated 19 May 2026. The page says an individual agreement can stipulate a minimum wage for the sector and that unions publish annual wage statistics.

Source: Business in Denmark (Virk, Ministry of Employment) - Pay · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employer ATP pension contribution (A-rate, monthly-paid employees)

198 kr per month (employee share 99 kr; total 297 kr) for employees working at least 117 hours a month; 132 kr for 78-116 hours; 66 kr for 39-77 hours; none below 39 hours

ATP A-rate: fixed amounts, not a percentage of pay. Page updated 19 May 2026; a linked PDF is titled ATP rates valid from 1 January 2024. Public employers may use other rates (B to F). Other employer charges are not covered.

Source: Virk (Danish Business Authority) - Gældende satser for ATP-bidrag · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employee labour market contribution (AM-bidrag)

AM-bidrag 8% of pay, withheld by the employer; young employees are exempt (see note)

From 1 January 2026 the employer does not report or pay AM-bidrag up to and including 31 December of the income year the employee turns 17. The employee ATP share is shown in the employer ATP fact.

Source: Skattestyrelsen (Skat.dk) - Beregn A-skat og am-bidrag af ansattes løn mm. · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

State income tax rates 2026 (bundskat, mellemskat, topskat, toptopskat)

Bundskat 12.01% on personal income above the personal allowance; mellemskat 7.5% over 641,200 kr; topskat 7.5% over 777,900 kr; toptopskat 5% over 2,592,700 kr (thresholds apply to personal income after AM-bidrag)

Valid from 1 January 2026

Rates in the table are cumulative: each higher tax is added on top of the lower ones for income above its threshold. State taxes only: municipal tax and, for members of the Church of Denmark, church tax are added. Personal allowance amount not covered.

Pay frequency

Monthly wages are paid monthly, in arrears; hourly, day and piecework wages are as a rule paid once or twice a month

Paid to the employee's NemKonto unless otherwise agreed. Page written by the Ministry of Employment, updated 19 May 2026.

Source: Business in Denmark (Virk, Ministry of Employment) - Pay · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Average municipal and church tax 2026

Average municipal tax 25.049% in 2026; average church tax 0.639% across all taxpayers (0.867% for members of the Church of Denmark)

Valid from 1 January 2026

Skatteministeriet table of average municipal and church tax percentages; actual rates differ by municipality. The 0.867% is the average for the roughly 75% of taxpayers who are church members.

Source: Skatteministeriet - Kommuneskatter - gennemsnitsprocenter 2007-2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Topskat renamed mellemskat; new topskat and toptopskat added

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

A tax reform introduces two new taxes from 2026 - topskat and toptopskat - and the former topskat is renamed mellemskat. For 2026: 7.5% mellemskat on personal income over 641,200 kr after AM-bidrag, 7.5% topskat over 777,900 kr, and 5% toptopskat over 2,592,700 kr.

For the employer

  • Confirm payroll software applies the new three-tier structure in 2026 withholding.

For the employee

  • Income above 777,900 kr after AM-bidrag bears both mellemskat and topskat; above 2,592,700 kr, toptopskat applies as well.

Source: Skattestyrelsen - Bundskat, mellemskat, topskat og toptopskat · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Barsel rights strengthened for the self-employed

Status: In force Jan 2026

Amendments to barselsloven passed by the Folketing on 29 April 2025 take effect in January 2026, letting self-employed people base their barselsdagpenge (parental leave benefit) on income from up to five years before starting their business, and allowing up to 3.5 hours of light administrative work per week during leave without losing the full benefit.

For the employer

  • Mainly relevant to HR teams managing contractors or owner-directors - confirm any self-employed associate on leave understands the new 3.5-hour weekly work allowance.

For the employee

  • Barselsdagpenge can now be calculated from pre-business income up to five years back, helping newer founders qualify for a realistic benefit level.
  • Up to 3.5 hours a week of light business admin is now allowed during leave without forfeiting full barselsdagpenge.

Source: Lund Elmer Sandager - Nye barselsregler for selvstændige fra 2026 · Payroll impact: Low: little or no payroll configuration needed · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

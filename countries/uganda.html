Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2026: PAYE tax-free threshold rises to UGX 335,000 a month

Status: In force 1 Jul 2026 High impact

The Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2026, assented to on 20 August 2026, applies from 1 July 2026 and raises the resident-individual tax-free threshold from UGX 2,820,000 to UGX 4,020,000 a year (UGX 235,000 to UGX 335,000 a month) and adds a 25% band before the 30% rate.

For the employer

  • Apply the new annual scale: nil to UGX 4,020,000; 20% to UGX 4,920,000; UGX 180,000 plus 25% to UGX 5,820,000; UGX 405,000 plus 30% above that; plus 10% on income over UGX 120,000,000.
  • Re-run July and August 2026 payroll on the new bands and reconcile any over-withheld PAYE with the Uganda Revenue Authority.

For the employee

  • Employees earning up to UGX 335,000 a month no longer pay PAYE; those above UGX 5,820,000 a year save UGX 165,000 a year.

In practice: Annual chargeable income UGX 4,920,000: 20% x UGX 900,000 = UGX 180,000.

The Act was assented to after the start date, so it applies retroactively to 1 July 2026. Source is a Ugandan law firm (KAA), matched by MRT Tax and by an Onyango Advocates summary of the same bands; the Act text and URA page could not be read.

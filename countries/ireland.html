National minimum wage

€14.15 per hour for ages 20 and over; age 19: €12.74; age 18: €11.32; under 18: €9.91

Valid from 1 January 2026

In force since 1 January 2026. Sub-minimum rates are 90%, 80% and 70% of the adult rate. Employees of a close relative and statutory apprentices are not entitled to the national minimum wage.

Source: Citizens Information - Minimum wage · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employee PRSI (Class A, private sector)

0% if weekly earnings are €352 or less; 4.35% on all earnings if over €352 a week from 1 October 2026 (4.2% up to 30 September 2026); tapered credit of up to €12 a week on €352.01 to €424

Valid from 1 October 2026

Class A only; other PRSI classes have different rates. The source says all PRSI contribution rates increase by 0.15% on 1 October 2026.

Source: Citizens Information - Paying social insurance (PRSI) · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employer PRSI (Class A, private sector)

From 1 October 2026: 9.15% on weekly earnings up to €552; 11.4% if weekly earnings are above €552 (from 1 January 2026 to 30 September 2026: 9% and 11.25%)

Valid from 1 October 2026

Class A only. The €552 weekly threshold applies from 1 January 2026. Other classes and low-earnings subclasses are not covered.

Source: Citizens Information - Paying social insurance (PRSI) · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Income tax rate bands 2026 (single person, no qualifying children)

20% on the first €44,000; 40% on the balance

Valid from 1 January 2026

Standard rate band for a single or widowed person without qualifying children. Other circumstances have different bands (for example married, one income: €53,000). Tax credits and USC are not included.

Universal Social Charge (USC), standard rates 2026

First €12,012 at 0.5%; next €16,688 at 2%; next €41,344 at 3%; balance at 8%

Valid from 1 January 2026

Standard rates and thresholds. Cumulative thresholds (computed from the source): €28,700 and €70,044. Reduced rates apply to some people and are shown on a separate Revenue page.

Source: Revenue Commissioners - Standard rates and thresholds of USC · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Tax credits unchanged; USC 2% band widens; PRSI rises from October

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

Budget 2026 keeps the personal, employee and earned-income tax credits at €2,000. From 1 January 2026 the 2% USC (Universal Social Charge) band increases by €1,318 to €28,700. Employer and employee PRSI (Pay Related Social Insurance) rates rise by 0.15% from 1 October 2026.

For the employer

  • Apply the new USC band from January and the PRSI increase from October 2026.

For the employee

  • Slight USC saving; small PRSI increase from October 2026.

Source: KPMG Ireland - Budget 2026 personal tax · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026 · Updated 3 October 2026

National Minimum Wage rises to €14.15

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The National Minimum Wage increases by 65c to €14.15 per hour from 1 January 2026.

For the employer

  • Raise any pay below €14.15/hour from the January 2026 pay run.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage workers receive 65c more per hour.

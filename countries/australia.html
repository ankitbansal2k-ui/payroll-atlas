🇦🇺 Australia: payroll law changes

Superannuation guarantee rate reaches 12%

Status: In force 1 Jul 2025

The superannuation guarantee (SG) percentage employers must contribute permanently increased to 12% from 1 July 2025.

For the employer

  • Calculate SG contributions at 12% of ordinary time earnings for pay periods from 1 July 2025.

For the employee

  • Employer super contributions on your earnings are made at 12%.

In practice: An employee with AUD 1,000 of ordinary time earnings attracts AUD 120 of SG contributions (1,000 x 12%).

Source: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries - Australia, Individual - Other taxes · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 30 September 2026

Payday Super: SG contributions must reach funds within seven business days of payday

Status: In force 1 Jul 2026 High impact

From 1 July 2026 employers must ensure superannuation guarantee contributions reach employees' funds within seven business days of payday.

For the employer

  • Pay super with each pay run so funds receive it within seven business days of payday.
  • Late contributions expose the employer to the superannuation guarantee charge.

For the employee

  • Super contributions arrive in your fund in line with each payday.

Replaces the quarterly SG contribution system.

Source: KPMG - Australia: Payday Superannuation legislation receives Royal Assent · Payroll impact: High: a new process, system, filing or calculation method · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 30 September 2026

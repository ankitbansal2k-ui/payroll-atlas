National daily minimum wage rises to GHS 21.77

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The National Tripartite Committee raised Ghana's national daily minimum wage by nine per cent, from GHS 19.97 to GHS 21.77, for 1 January to 31 December 2026.

For the employer

  • Check that no employee is paid below GHS 21.77 per day.
  • The SSNIT minimum insurable earning for 2026 also rose, following the minimum wage increase.

For the employee

  • Statutory floor for daily pay is GHS 21.77 from 1 January 2026.

Announced by the National Tripartite Committee (Finance Minister's statement reported by the Ghanaian Times, 11 November 2025) as valid 1 January to 31 December 2026; the Fair Wages and Salaries Commission notice was not read. SSNIT's 2026 notice confirms the minimum wage rose but does not state the amount.

Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2026: new monthly PAYE bands, first GHS 588 tax-free

Status: In force 1 Sep 2026 High impact

GRA says the Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2026 (Act 1178) took effect on 1 September 2026 and published new monthly PAYE bands for resident individuals: first GHS 588 at 0%, then 5%, 10%, 17.5%, 25%, 30% and 35% on income above GHS 50,000 a month.

For the employer

  • Recompute PAYE with the monthly bands: next GHS 80 at 5%, next GHS 100 at 10%, next GHS 2,900 at 17.5%, next GHS 16,000 at 25%, next GHS 30,332 at 30%, above GHS 50,000 at 35%.
  • Apply the new bands to payroll from 1 September 2026 and correct any September PAYE already run on the old bands.

For the employee

  • Annual equivalent: first GHS 7,056 is tax-free.

In practice: Monthly chargeable income GHS 1,000: GHS 80 x 5% + GHS 100 x 10% + GHS 232 x 17.5% = GHS 54.60.

Effective date and bands come from GRA's own notice on Act 1178 and its PAYE page (year of assessment 2026); the Act text was not read, so check the Act and any GRA guidance for transitional treatment.

SSNIT maximum monthly contribution raised to GHS 9,315

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

From 1 January 2026 the maximum insurable earning for SSNIT is GHS 69,000 (up from GHS 61,000) and the minimum is GHS 587.80 (up from GHS 539.19), so the maximum and minimum contributions payable are GHS 9,315.00 and GHS 79.40.

For the employer

  • Update the SSNIT insurable earnings cap to GHS 69,000 and the floor to GHS 587.80 in payroll.
  • Pay the right contributions: maximum GHS 9,315.00 and minimum GHS 79.40 from 1 January 2026.

For the employee

  • Pensionable earnings above GHS 69,000 do not attract further SSNIT contributions in 2026.

SSNIT says the cap was adjusted with the National Pensions Regulatory Authority under section 63(3) of the National Pensions Act 2008 (Act 766), and the floor followed the rise in the National Daily Minimum Wage.

Statutory national minimum wage

No statutory national minimum wage (Eurostat, 1 July 2026); minimum pay is laid down by collective agreements for specific sectors

Eurostat lists Italy with Denmark, Austria, Finland and Sweden as EU countries without a national minimum wage on 1 July 2026.

Source: Eurostat - Minimum wage statistics · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

INPS contribution ceiling and floors 2026 (employees)

Annual ceiling of the contribution and pensionable base €122,295 (for those first insured after 31 December 1995 or who opt for the contributive system); additional 1% employee contribution above €56,224 a year (€4,685 a month); minimum daily pay for contributions €58.13

Valid from 1 January 2026

INPS circular no. 6 of 30 January 2026 on values for employees in private and public schemes. The ceiling is stated as €122,295.40, rounded to €122,295.

Source: INPS - Circolare n. 6 del 30 gennaio 2026: Determinazione per l'anno 2026 del limite minimo di retribuzione giornaliera e aggiornamento degli altri valori · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

National income tax (IRPEF) rates 2026

23% up to €28,000; 33% from €28,001 to €50,000; 43% over €50,000

Valid from 1 January 2026

National IRPEF only. Regional and municipal surcharges depend on place of residence; deductions and tax credits are not included. For total income above €200,000 the benefit of the lower 33% rate is neutralised (Law 199/2025).

IRPEF middle rate set at 33% for 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026 Effective income year 2026

For 2026 IRPEF is 23% up to €28,000, 33% from €28,001 to €50,000 and 43% above €50,000.

For the employer

  • Update withholding tables for the 33% middle bracket.

For the employee

  • Middle earners pay less IRPEF.

Pay transparency decree in force

Status: In force 7 Jun 2026 High impact

Legislative Decree 7 May 2026, No. 96, published on 1 June 2026, implements EU Directive 2023/970 on pay transparency and entered into force on 7 June 2026.

For the employer

  • Review recruitment, pay-structure and reporting processes against the decree's transparency obligations.

For the employee

  • New rights to pay transparency under the decree.

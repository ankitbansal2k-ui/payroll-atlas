IRPEF middle rate set at 33% for 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026 Effective income year 2026

For 2026 IRPEF is 23% up to €28,000, 33% from €28,001 to €50,000 and 43% above €50,000.

For the employer

  • Update withholding tables for the 33% middle bracket.

For the employee

  • Middle earners pay less IRPEF.

Source: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries - Italy, taxes on personal income · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026 · Updated 3 October 2026

Pay transparency decree in force

Status: In force 7 Jun 2026 High impact

Legislative Decree 7 May 2026, No. 96, published on 1 June 2026, implements EU Directive 2023/970 on pay transparency and entered into force on 7 June 2026.

For the employer

  • Review recruitment, pay-structure and reporting processes against the decree's transparency obligations.

For the employee

  • New rights to pay transparency under the decree.

Source: Normattiva - D.Lgs. 7 maggio 2026, n. 96 · Payroll impact: High: a new process, system, filing or calculation method · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 30 September 2026

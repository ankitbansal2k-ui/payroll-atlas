🇵🇭 Philippines: payroll law changes

3 tracked changes · APAC · sources last checked 1 October 2026

Statutory and legislative payroll changes in Philippines, each linked to the page it was checked against. For information only, not legal or tax advice: confirm details with the source before acting. See the terms. See the Glossary for abbreviations.

SSS contribution rate rises to 15%; MSC ceiling to PHP 35,000

Status: In force 1 Jan 2025

Under Republic Act No. 11199, SSS (Social Security System) contributions rose from 14% to 15% on 1 January 2025, split 10% employer and 5% employee; the Monthly Salary Credit range moved to PHP 5,000-35,000 (from PHP 4,000-30,000).

For the employer

  • Contribute 10% of the applicable Monthly Salary Credit and withhold 5% from the employee.
  • Apply the new MSC minimum of PHP 5,000 and maximum of PHP 35,000.

For the employee

  • Employee SSS share is 5% of the Monthly Salary Credit.

In practice: At the PHP 35,000 maximum MSC, the employer pays PHP 3,500 and the employee PHP 1,750 (total PHP 5,250).

NCR minimum wage rises by PHP 60 to PHP 755 a day (Wage Order NCR-28)

Status: In force 26 Sep 2026

Wage Order No. NCR-28, issued on 7 September 2026 and published on 11 September 2026, grants a PHP 60 per day basic wage increase in the National Capital Region from 26 September 2026: non-agriculture PHP 695 to PHP 755 a day, and agriculture PHP 658 to PHP 718 a day. The same PHP 718 rate is listed for service and retail establishments employing 15 workers or fewer and manufacturing establishments regularly employing fewer than 10 workers.

For the employer

  • Raise NCR employees paid below PHP 755 a day (PHP 718 in agriculture and the small service, retail and manufacturing categories) from 26 September 2026 in the daily rate used in payroll.
  • Check whether an exemption category applies and whether an exemption application is made; the NWPC site lists application forms for NCR-28.

For the employee

  • NCR minimum-wage earners receive PHP 60 more per day.

NWPC lists NCR-28 as the current NCR daily minimum wage (baseline PHP 695 under NCR-26). It also lists the earlier Wage Order No. NCR-27 (effective 25 July 2026, PHP 755 then PHP 780 from 20 January 2027) as subject to continuing litigation, with a notice that its implementation awaits a final resolution from the Pasig and Navotas courts. Confirm with the RTWPB-NCR which order applies to your establishment, especially for the January 2027 step.

Source: NWPC (DOLE) - National Capital Region current daily minimum wage rates · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 6 October 2026

NCR domestic worker minimum wage rises to PHP 7,800 a month

Status: In force 7 Feb 2026

Wage Order No. NCR-DW-06 raises the monthly minimum wage for domestic workers (kasambahay) in the National Capital Region by PHP 800, from PHP 7,000 to PHP 7,800, effective 7 February 2026.

For the employer

  • Pay NCR household employers' domestic workers at least PHP 7,800 a month from 7 February 2026.

For the employee

  • NCR domestic workers receive PHP 800 more per month.

NWPC states the effective date as 7 February 2026. NWPC pages give slightly different publication dates (21 January in a news item, 22 January on the rates page), so none is stated here.

Source: NWPC (DOLE) - RTWPB-NCR wage order for kasambahays to take effect on February 07 · Payroll impact: Low: little or no payroll configuration needed · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 6 October 2026

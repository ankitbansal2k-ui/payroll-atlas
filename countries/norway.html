Key facts

Currency: NOK

Check the source before use. For information only, not legal or tax advice.

Minimum wage (legal minimums in nine sectors only)

No set minimum wage in other sectors; legal minimum wages apply in nine sectors through generalised collective agreements (allmenngjøring), and wages elsewhere are agreed individually or in collective agreements

Sector rates (e.g. construction, cleaning, hospitality) are on the source page. The page also presents a new sector, automotive, with minimums from 15 June 2026 (e.g. NOK 208 an hour for unskilled workers aged 18 or over).

Source: Arbeidstilsynet (Norwegian Labour Inspection Authority) - Minimum wage · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employer's national insurance contribution (arbeidsgiveravgift) 2026, general trades

Zone I 14.1%; Ia 10.6% (14.1% above the NOK 850,000 exemption amount); II 10.6%; III 6.4%; IV 5.1%; IVa 7.9%; V 0%

The source says there were no changes to zones or rates from 2025 to 2026 (issued 6 January 2026). Different rules apply to central government, health trusts, sector-excluded activities and firms in financial difficulty. Rates vary by where the employer is located.

Source: Skatteetaten - Arbeidsgiveravgift til folketrygden for 2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employee national insurance contribution (trygdeavgift) 2026

7.6% on wage income; lower limit NOK 99,650

Other rates listed in the same table: fishing, trapping and childcare 7.6%; other business income 10.8%; pension income 5.1%. The source does not state an upper income limit.

Source: Skatteetaten - Forskuddsutskrivingen 2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Bracket tax (trinnskatt) 2026

1.7% on income above NOK 226,100; 4.0% above 318,300; 13.7% above 725,050; 16.8% above 980,100; 17.8% above 1,467,200

Applies to personal income (personinntekt) in addition to 22% tax on ordinary income and trygdeavgift. Personal allowance (class 1) NOK 114,540. Finnmark and parts of Troms have a lower fellesskatt rate (4.75% instead of 8.25%).

National Insurance basic amount (grunnbeløp, G)

NOK 136,549 from 1 May 2026, up from NOK 130,160 (an increase of 4.91%)

Valid from 1 May 2026

The source says the amount is formally adopted on 22 May 2026. Benefits adjusted through G rise by the same 4.91% from 1 May.

Source: Nav - Regulering av grunnbeløpet og pengestøtter fra 1. mai 2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

2026 bracket tax (trinnskatt), personal allowance and trygdeavgift

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

For 2026 trinnskatt applies at 1.7% above 226,100 kr, 4.0% above 318,300 kr, 13.7% above 725,050 kr, 16.8% above 980,100 kr and 17.8% above 1,467,200 kr. The class 1 personal allowance is 114,540 kr and trygdeavgift on wage income is 7.6%.

For the employer

  • Retrieve updated 2026 tax cards and load the 2026 tables before the first pay run.

For the employee

  • Tax cards are issued automatically by Skatteetaten.

Source: Skatteetaten - Forskuddsutskrivingen 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Employer NI zone rates and boundaries unchanged for 2026

Status: In force 2026 Confirmed for 2026

Skatteetaten confirmed no changes to zones or rates from 2025 to 2026: 14.1% (zone I), 10.6% (II), 6.4% (III), 5.1% (IV), 7.9% (IVa) and 0% (V). Zone Ia is 10.6% up to an exemption amount of 850,000 kr, then 14.1%.

For the employer

  • Confirm each employee's zone in the a-melding (The a-melding - monthly report to NAV, SSB and the Tax Administration (Norwegian)) remains correct.

For the employee

  • Employer NI is an employer cost and does not reduce net pay.

In practice: 700,000 kr of wages in zone III: 700,000 x 6.4% = 44,800 kr; in zone I: 700,000 x 14.1% = 98,700 kr.

Source: Skatteetaten - Arbeidsgiveravgift til folketrygden for 2026 · Payroll impact: Low: little or no payroll configuration needed · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026 · Updated 4 October 2026

Grunnbeløp rises 4.91% from 1 May 2026

Status: In force 1 May 2026

The National Insurance basic amount (G) rose from 130,160 kr to 136,549 kr from 1 May 2026, an increase of 6,389 kr or 4.91%.

For the employer

  • Update any G-based thresholds used in payroll or HR systems from May 2026.

For the employee

  • Benefits expressed as multiples of G are adjusted upward.

In practice: A 6G benefit cap: 6 x 136,549 = 819,294 kr a year.

Source: NAV - Regulering 2026 · Payroll impact: Low: little or no payroll configuration needed · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

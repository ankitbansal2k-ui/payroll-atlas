🇱🇧 Lebanon: payroll law changes

2 tracked changes · MENAT · sources last checked 1 October 2026

NSSF contribution ceilings raised

Status: In force Aug 2025

The NSSF maternity and sickness ceiling rose to LL 120 million per month in August 2025, and the family benefit ceiling was revised to LL 18 million in July 2025.

For the employer

  • Maternity and sickness: 8% on up to LL 120 million per month.
  • Family benefits: 6% on up to LL 18 million per month.
  • End-of-service indemnity: 5% of total annual earnings, no ceiling.

For the employee

  • Medical scheme: 3% on up to LL 120 million per month.

In practice: Employer maternity and sickness at the ceiling: 120,000,000 x 8% = LL 9,600,000 per month.

The previous monthly ceiling, set in April 2024, was LL 90 million.

Official minimum wage raised to LL 28 million a month (Decree 699)

Status: In force 1 Aug 2025

Decree No. 699 of 18 July 2025 (Official Gazette No. 32, 24 July 2025) sets the new official minimum wage for employees and workers covered by the Labour Law. In NSSF Memorandum No. 801 of 1 August 2025, the NSSF applies a minimum monthly wage of LL 28,000,000 and a minimum daily wage of LL 1,300,000 for calculating employers' contributions from 1 August 2025.

For the employer

  • Raise employees paid below LL 28,000,000 a month (LL 1,300,000 a day) to the new minimum.
  • Calculate NSSF contributions on at least the new minimum wage from 1 August 2025.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage employees under the Labour Law are entitled to LL 28 million a month.

NSSF Memorandum No. 801 first set the sickness and maternity ceiling at LL 140 million, but Memorandum No. 805 of 22 August 2025 (under Decree No. 887) replaced it with LL 120 million.

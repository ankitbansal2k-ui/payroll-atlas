Key facts

Currency: PLN

Check the source before use. For information only, not legal or tax advice.

Minimum monthly wage and minimum hourly rate

4,806 zł gross per month; minimum hourly rate 31.40 zł (from 1 January 2026)

Valid from 1 January 2026

Set by the Council of Ministers regulation of 11 September 2025 (Dz.U. 2025 item 1242), in force from 1 January 2026.

Source: Dziennik Ustaw - Rozporządzenie Rady Ministrów z dnia 11 września 2025 r. w sprawie wysokości minimalnego wynagrodzenia za pracę (poz. 1242) · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employer social security contributions (employees on an employment contract)

Pension 9.76%; disability 6.50%; accident 0.67% to 3.33% (1.67% for small firms employing up to 9 people); Labour Fund 2.45%; Guaranteed Employee Benefits Fund (FGSP) 0.10%

Valid from 1 January 2026

Rates as a percentage of gross pay, as listed on the page for an employee on the 2026 minimum wage. Pension and disability apply only up to the annual cap (see ssCeiling).

Source: Biznes.gov.pl - Ile kosztuje zatrudnienie pracownika na umowę o pracę · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employee social security and health contributions

Pension 9.76%; disability 1.50%; sickness 2.45%; health insurance 9%

Valid from 1 January 2026

Percentages of gross pay as listed on the page. The three social contributions add up to 13.71% (computed). Health insurance is charged on pay after the employee's social contributions (page's worked example).

Source: Biznes.gov.pl - Ile kosztuje zatrudnienie pracownika na umowę o pracę · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Annual cap for pension and disability contributions 2026

282,600 zł per year (30 times the average monthly wage): no pension or disability contributions once annual pay exceeds this limit

Valid from 1 January 2026

ZUS also gives 282,600 zł for 2026. Sickness, accident, Labour Fund and health contributions are not covered by this cap.

Source: Biznes.gov.pl - Ile kosztuje zatrudnienie pracownika na umowę o pracę · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Personal income tax scale (skala podatkowa) 2026

12% on annual taxable income up to 120,000 zł, less the amount decreasing tax of 3,600 zł; above 120,000 zł: 10,800 zł plus 32% of the excess

Valid from 1 January 2026

The 3,600 zł reduction equals 12% of the 30,000 zł tax-free amount (per biznes.gov.pl). Health contributions are not deductible. Other regimes (flat 19%, lump sum) for business income are not covered.

Pay frequency

At least once a month, on a fixed date set in advance, paid in arrears

The employer must tell the employee in writing about the pay frequency within 7 days of concluding the employment contract.

Source: Biznes.gov.pl - Ile kosztuje zatrudnienie pracownika na umowę o pracę · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Minimum wage rises to 4,806 zł

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

Under the Council of Ministers regulation of 11 September 2025, the minimum monthly wage from 1 January 2026 is 4,806 zł and the minimum hourly rate is 31.40 zł.

For the employer

  • Raise any employee below 4,806 zł a month (or 31.40 zł an hour where the hourly minimum applies) from January 2026.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage workers receive the increase automatically.

Source: Zielona Linia (gov.pl) - Wynagrodzenie minimalne w 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 30 September 2026

Recruitment pay transparency in force; full pay transparency law not before 2027

Status: In force 24 Dec 2025 High impact

Labour Code recruitment transparency requirements have applied since 24 December 2025. The full law implementing Directive 2023/970 (revised draft of 4 May 2026) will enter into force 6 months after publication, no earlier than early 2027.

For the employer

  • Apply recruitment-stage pay transparency rules now and prepare pay structures and gender pay gap reporting for 2027.

Source: SD Worx - Jawność wynagrodzeń dopiero od 2027 roku · Payroll impact: High: a new process, system, filing or calculation method · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 30 September 2026

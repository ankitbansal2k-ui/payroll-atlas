🇩🇴 Dominican Republic: payroll law changes

3 tracked changes · LATAM · sources last checked 1 October 2026

Statutory and legislative payroll changes in Dominican Republic, each linked to the page it was checked against. For information only, not legal or tax advice: confirm details with the source before acting. See the terms. See the Glossary for abbreviations.

TSS raises social security contribution caps

Status: In force 1 Feb 2026

The Tesorería de la Seguridad Social (TSS) sets new contribution caps for the Contributory Regime from 1 February 2026, following the private-sector wage increase in Resolution CNS-01-2025, using a minimum wage reference of RD$23,223.00: Work Risk Insurance RD$92,892.00, Family Health Insurance RD$232,230.00, and Old Age, Disability and Survivor Insurance RD$464,460.00.

For the employer

  • Apply the new contribution caps for Work Risk, Family Health and pension insurance from 1 February 2026.

For the employee

  • Health and pension contributions are calculated up to the new higher caps.

Private-sector minimum wage up 8% from 1 February 2026 (large firms RD$29,988)

Status: In force 1 Feb 2026

From 1 February 2026 the minimum wage of private-sector workers in micro, small, medium and large companies rose 8%, as approved by the Comite Nacional de Salarios: RD$29,988.00 a month in large companies, RD$27,489.60 in medium, RD$18,421.20 in small and RD$16,993.20 in micro companies.

For the employer

  • Raise unsectorised private-sector employees below the new monthly minimum for their company size from the February 2026 pay run.
  • Update any pay scales or allowances that reference the minimum wage.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage workers receive the 8% increase from 1 February 2026; the Labour Ministry puts the rise between February 2025 and February 2026 at 20% nominal.

Sector minimums (free zones, hotels, casinos, restaurants) are set by separate resolutions and apply from 1 June 2026. Security guards in private guard companies and field workers have their own minimums, not covered here.

Free-zone, hotel and restaurant minimum wages rise from 1 June 2026

Status: In force 1 Jun 2026

Resolutions CNS-03-2025 and CNS-04-2025 of the Comite Nacional de Salarios set 2026 sector minimums valid from 1 June 2026: free zones RD$20,875.00 a month; hotels and casinos RD$21,840.00 (151 workers or more) or RD$18,409.30 (150 or fewer); food and beverage establishments RD$21,000.00 (151 or more) or RD$17,701.25 (150 or fewer).

For the employer

  • Move free-zone, hotel, casino and restaurant staff below these monthly minimums up to them from 1 June 2026.

For the employee

  • Sector minimum-wage workers receive the new rates from 1 June 2026.

Source is an EY Central America tax alert dated 3 March 2026 (professional source) summarising Resolutions CNS-03-2025 (30 April 2025) and CNS-04-2025 (26 May 2025); the resolution texts were not fetched.

SMIC (salaire minimum de croissance)

€12.31 gross per hour; €1,867.02 gross per month for a full-time (35-hour) job, from 1 June 2026 (previously €12.02 per hour / €1,823.03 per month)

Valid from 1 June 2026

Revalued by 2.41% on 1 June 2026 (arrêté of 22 May 2026) per the source. Applies in mainland France and the overseas territories listed by the source; Mayotte has a lower rate (€9.56 per hour).

Source: Service-Public.gouv.fr - Salaire minimum : le Smic va augmenter le 1er juin · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employer social security contributions (private sector, headline rates)

Health/maternity/disability/death: reduced 7% or full 13%; old-age 2.11% on total pay plus 8.55% within the ceiling; family allowances: reduced 3.45% or full 5.25%; unemployment 4.00% and AGS 0.25%, both within €192,240 in 2026; CSA 0.30%; Fnal 0.10% (under 50 employees)

Valid from 1 January 2026

Urssaf table "Taux patronaux applicables au secteur privé", updated 1 January 2026. Not covered: accident at work (rate notified by Carsat), versement mobilité, Fnal 0.50% (50+ employees), 0.016% social dialogue contribution, training levies.

Source: Urssaf - Taux de cotisations - Secteur prive · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employee social security contributions (private sector, headline rates)

Old-age 0.40% on total pay plus 6.90% within the ceiling; CSG imposable 2.40% and CSG non imposable 6.80%, plus CRDS 0.50%, each on 98.25% of gross pay up to €192,240 in 2026; extra health contribution 1.30% in Haut-Rhin, Bas-Rhin and Moselle

Valid from 1 January 2026

Urssaf table "Taux salariaux applicables au secteur privé", updated 1 January 2026. The table lists no employee unemployment contribution. CSG imposable / non imposable are the page's own labels.

Source: Urssaf - Taux de cotisations - Secteur prive · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Social security ceiling (PMSS / PASS) 2026

€4,005 per month; €48,060 per year; €12,015 per quarter; €924 per week; €220 per day; €30 per hour (from 1 January 2026, up from €3,925 per month in 2025)

Valid from 1 January 2026

Urssaf's 2026 table also quotes a separate limit of €192,240 for unemployment, AGS, CSG and CRDS bases.

Source: Service-Public.gouv.fr - Securite sociale : quel sera le plafond annuel en 2026 ? · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Income tax scale (barème de l'impot sur le revenu), per 1 part of family quotient

Per 1 part of family quotient (2026 scale, applied to 2025 income): up to €11,600: 0%; €11,601 to €29,579: 11%; €29,580 to €84,577: 30%; €84,578 to €181,917: 41%; over €181,917: 45%

Pay frequency

Monthly: salary is obligatorily paid once a month (employees not on a monthly salary, such as seasonal, temporary or home workers, must be paid at least twice a month, at least every 15 days)

Source: Service-Public.gouv.fr - Paiement du salaire · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Social security ceiling (PMSS) rises 2% to €4,005 a month

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The monthly social security ceiling rises to €4,005 (from €3,925 in 2025), a 2% increase; the annual ceiling (PASS (Monthly value of the plafond de la Sécurité sociale (French social security ceiling))) is €48,060. It is the base for capped contributions and for maximum social security benefits.

For the employer

  • Update the PMSS/PASS in payroll from the January 2026 pay run so capped contributions are calculated on €4,005.

For the employee

  • Capped contributions and maximum daily sick-pay benefits rise with the ceiling.

In practice: €4,005 × 12 = €48,060 annual ceiling.

Source: Service-Public.fr - Plafond de la sécurité sociale 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 30 September 2026

SMIC revalued twice: 1 January and 1 June 2026

Status: In force 1 Jun 2026 Effective 1 Jan and 1 Jun 2026

The SMIC (Salaire minimum interprofessionnel de croissance (French) - minimum wage) was €12.02 gross an hour from 1 January 2026 (€1,823.03 a month on a 35-hour week), then a further 2.41% on 1 June 2026 to €12.31 an hour (€1,867.02 a month).

For the employer

  • Raise any pay below €12.31/hour from June 2026 and review minimum-linked calculations.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage earners received two increases in 2026.

New private-sector minimum wage order in force from 1 January 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026 High impact

Government Notice No. 605A of 2025 (Labour Institutions (Minimum Wage for Private Sector) Order) took effect on 1 January 2026 and replaced the 2022 wage order with new sector and sub-sector minimum wages.

For the employer

  • Raise any private-sector pay below the new sector minimum; employers paying more must keep paying the higher wage.
  • Employers in sectors with no wage board must pay at least TZS 175,000 a month, up from TZS 150,000.

For the employee

  • Employees also gain a leave travel allowance once every two years of continuous service and a subsistence allowance when sent to work outside their workstation.

Rates differ by sector and sub-sector; check the table in the Wage Order for the exact minimum. Source is a law firm (Clyde & Co), corroborated by ALN Tanzania (published 13 October 2025) and Daily News; the Government Notice itself could not be reached.

Key facts

Currency: HUF

Check the source before use. For information only, not legal or tax advice.

Minimum wage (minimálbér) and guaranteed minimum wage (garantált bérminimum)

Minimum wage HUF 322,800 a month (HUF 1,856 an hour) for full-time work; guaranteed minimum wage HUF 373,200 a month (HUF 2,145 an hour) for jobs requiring at least secondary education or qualification

Valid from 1 January 2026

Government Decree 426/2025 (XII. 23.), applicable from 1 January 2026. Weekly and daily rates are also set: HUF 74,210 and 14,850 (minimum); HUF 85,800 and 17,160 (guaranteed).

Source: Nemzeti Jogszabálytár (Magyar Közlöny) - 426/2025. (XII. 23.) Korm. rendelet a kötelező legkisebb munkabér (minimálbér) és a garantált bérminimum megállapításáról · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Social contribution tax (szociális hozzájárulási adó) paid by the employer

13% of the tax base

NAV information booklet published 16 January 2026: the rate of the tax is 13% of the tax base. Other rates apply to some items (for example long-term investment contract income at 0%, 8% or 13%). Tax base rules, exemptions and other employer charges are not covered.

Source: NAV (Nemzeti Adó- és Vámhivatal) - A szociális hozzájárulási adó (információs füzet) · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Social security contribution paid by the insured person (járulék)

Társadalombiztosítási járulék 18.5%; the 10% pension contribution is payable only for jobseekers receiving allowance and by church legal entities for church personnel in church service

NAV page "Fizetendő járulékok 2026. január 1-jétől" (dated 5 January 2026) lists the contributions payable by the insured person from 1 July 2020. Contribution base and special cases are not covered.

Source: NAV (Nemzeti Adó- és Vámhivatal) - Fizetendő járulékok 2026. január 1-jétől · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Personal income tax (szja) rate

15% of the tax base, unless the Act provides otherwise

Szja Act (1995. évi CXVII.) section 8 (1), consolidated text on the Nemzeti Jogszabálytár dated 1 October 2026. NAV's rate table also lists 15% from 2016 for the consolidated tax base. Allowances and credits are not covered.

Minimum wage rises 11%, guaranteed wage minimum 7%

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

From 1 January 2026 the minimálbér rises 11% to 322,800 Ft a month (1,856 Ft/hour) and the garantált bérminimum rises 7% to 373,200 Ft a month (2,145 Ft/hour), following agreement with employer and employee representatives.

For the employer

  • Raise pay for affected employees from the January 2026 pay run.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage and guaranteed-minimum earners receive raises.

Source: BDO Hungary - Minimálbér és garantált bérminimum 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Entrepreneurs' minimum szocho base cut to 100% of minimum wage

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

From 1 January 2026 the 112.5% multiplier for primary-occupation sole and corporate entrepreneurs is removed: their monthly social contribution tax (szocho) base is at least 100% of the minimum wage or guaranteed wage minimum. Sole entrepreneurs taxed on business income may now file quarterly.

For the employer

  • Entrepreneurs should update their minimum szocho base.

For the employee

  • No change to employee deductions from this measure.

Source: NAV (Hungarian Tax Administration) - Csökken a szociális hozzájárulási adó alapja · Payroll impact: Low: little or no payroll configuration needed · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

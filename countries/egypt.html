🇪🇬 Egypt: payroll law changes

3 tracked changes · MENAT · sources last checked 1 October 2026

Private-sector minimum wage raised to EGP 7,000

Effective 1 March 2025

The National Wages Council raised the private-sector minimum wage from EGP 6,000 to EGP 7,000, effective 1 March 2025.

For the employer

  • Pay at least EGP 7,000 per month to private-sector workers.
  • Periodic allowance of at least 3% of the insurance subscription wage and not less than EGP 250 per month.
  • Part-time workers: net hourly wage of at least EGP 28.

For the employee

  • Minimum monthly wage of EGP 7,000.

Source: Ministry of Planning, Economic Development and International Cooperation - First meeting of the National Wages Council · Last verified 1 October 2026

Social insurance minimum and maximum insurable wage for 2026

Effective 1 January 2026

Insurable wage limits rise 15% each year through 2028. For 2026 the minimum is EGP 2,700 and the maximum EGP 16,700 per month.

For the employer

  • Employer contribution: 18.75% of the social insurance salary.

For the employee

  • Employee contribution: 11% of the social insurance salary.

In practice: Employee at the 2026 maximum: 16,700 x 11% = EGP 1,837 per month.

Source: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries - Egypt, Individual, Other taxes · Last verified 1 October 2026

Insurable wage limits rise again in 2027

Effective 1 January 2027 Upcoming

Under the scheduled annual increases, the minimum insurable wage becomes EGP 3,200 and the maximum EGP 19,300 per month in 2027.

For the employer

  • 18.75% employer contribution applies up to EGP 19,300.

For the employee

  • 11% employee contribution applies up to EGP 19,300.

In practice: Employee at the 2027 maximum: 19,300 x 11% = EGP 2,123 per month.

Source: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries - Egypt, Individual, Other taxes · Last verified 1 October 2026

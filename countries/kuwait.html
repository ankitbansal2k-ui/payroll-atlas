Social security contributions for Kuwaiti nationals: employer 11.5%, employee 10.5%

Status: Ongoing Current rates

Social security contributions apply to Kuwaiti national employees. The employer contributes 11.5% and the employee 8% of monthly salary up to a ceiling of KWD 2,750, and employees pay an additional 2.5% supplementary contribution (on salary up to KWD 1,500), for a total employee rate of 10.5%.

For the employer

  • Contribute 11.5% of monthly salary for Kuwaiti national employees, up to the KWD 2,750 monthly ceiling.
  • Withhold the employee's 8% basic contribution and 2.5% supplementary contribution and remit them to the Public Institution for Social Security.

For the employee

  • Kuwaiti nationals contribute 8% basic plus 2.5% supplementary (10.5% total), subject to the applicable ceilings.

Expatriate workers are not covered by the social security scheme; they are generally entitled to end-of-service indemnity under Kuwait Labour Law.

Central Bank launches Kuwait Wage Payment System (KWPS) for private-sector wages

Status: In force 28 Sep 2026

On 28 September 2026 the Central Bank of Kuwait (CBK) announced the launch of the Kuwait Wage Payment System (KWPS), a national platform for processing and monitoring wage transfers for private-sector employees through local banks so that wages are disbursed electronically. CBK manages the system technically and operationally, while the Public Authority for Manpower oversees employers' compliance with the KWPS provisions.

For the employer

  • Expect private-sector wage transfers to be processed and monitored through the KWPS via local banks; ask your bank and the Public Authority for Manpower how your payroll file must be submitted.
  • The system lets supervisory authorities monitor wage disbursement in real time and detect non-compliance early, so late or missing wage payments become visible to the regulator.

For the employee

  • Private-sector wages are paid electronically through local banks and tracked on a national platform.

The press release announces the launch only; it does not state employer deadlines, file formats or penalties, so none are stated here. It is the third system under phase II of the Kuwait National Payments System project.

Employee social insurance contributions (headline rates)

Health 7.3% (half of 14.6%) plus half of the fund's additional rate (TK 2.69%: 1.345%; average 2.9%); pension 9.3%; unemployment 1.3%; long-term care 1.8% with 1 child, 2.4% childless, and 1.55% / 1.3% / 1.05% / 0.8% with 2 / 3 / 4 / 5+ children under 25; Saxony differs

Valid from 1 January 2026

Rates for 2026. Care insurance: childless surcharge of 0.6 points is borne by the employee (SGB XI s.58); reduction of 0.25 points per child from the 2nd to 5th child under 25 (SGB XI s.55). Employer share stays 1.8% in the TK table.

Social insurance contribution ceilings (Beitragsbemessungsgrenzen) 2026

Pension and unemployment insurance: €8,450 per month (€101,400 per year); health and long-term care: €5,812.50 per month (€69,750 per year); knappschaftliche pension: €10,400 per month (€124,800 per year); compulsory-insurance threshold (health/care): €6,450 per month (€77,400 per year)

Valid from 1 January 2026

Sozialversicherungsrechengrößen-Verordnung 2026 of 24 Nov 2025 (BGBl. 2025 I Nr. 278), in force 1 Jan 2026. Unemployment insurance uses the general pension ceiling (SGB III s.341(4)).

Mindestlohn rises to €13.90; Minijob limit to €603

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The statutory minimum wage has been €13.90 gross per hour since 1 January 2026 and will rise to €14.60 in 2027. The Minijob earnings limit rose accordingly to €603 a month from 1 January 2026 and rises to €633 in 2027.

For the employer

  • Check hourly rates and Minijob hours so pay stays at or above €13.90 and within the €603 limit.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage workers receive a raise; a further increase to €14.60 follows in 2027.

Source: Bundesregierung - Mindestlohn steigt · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Social insurance contribution ceilings raised for 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The 2026 contribution assessment ceiling for pension insurance is €8,450 a month; for statutory health insurance it is €69,750 a year (€5,812.50 a month). The compulsory insurance threshold for health and long-term care insurance is €6,450 a month (€77,400 a year). The values reflect 5.16% wage growth in 2024.

For the employer

  • Update ceilings in payroll so contributions are calculated up to the new limits.

For the employee

  • Higher earners pay contributions on a larger share of salary.

Source: Deutsche Rentenversicherung - Rechengrößen der Sozialversicherung 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Salario básico unificado USD 482 for 2026

2026

The Consejo Nacional de Trabajo y Salarios agreed by consensus to set the 2026 unified basic wage at USD 482.

For the employer

  • Pay at least USD 482 per month in 2026.

For the employee

  • Basic wage is USD 482 per month.

Source: Ministerio del Trabajo - Acuerdan fijar el Salario Básico Unificado de 2026 en USD 482 · Last verified 1 October 2026

2026 personal income tax table: zero-rate band to USD 12,208

Tax year 2026

The 2026 personal income tax table taxes annual income up to USD 12,208 at 0%, with a top rate of 37% on income from USD 109,956.

For the employer

  • Use the 2026 table when computing employee withholding.

For the employee

  • 0% on annual income up to USD 12,208.
  • 37% top rate from USD 109,956.

Source: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries - Ecuador, Individual, Taxes on personal income · Last verified 1 October 2026

Key facts

Currency: SEK

Statutory minimum wage

No statutory minimum wage; wages are often regulated by collective agreements

The source page is the Swedish Work Environment Authority's guidance for posted workers.

Source: Swedish Work Environment Authority (Arbetsmiljöverket) - The rights of posted workers in Sweden · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employer contributions (arbetsgivaravgifter) 2026

Full rate 31.42% of pay (born 1959 or later): sickness 3.55%, parental 2.00%, old-age pension 10.21%, survivors 0.30%, labour market 2.64%, work injury 0.10%, general payroll 12.62%. Born 1938 to 1958: 10.21%. Born 1937 or earlier: none

Valid from 1 January 2026

Temporary reduced rate 20.81% on pay up to 25,000 kr a month, for employees born 2003 to 2007 (the 2026 group), on pay from 1 April 2026 to 30 September 2027. Pay above 25,000 kr a month is charged at the full 31.42%.

Source: Skatteverket - Arbetsgivaravgifter · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employee general pension fee (allmän pensionsavgift) 2026

7% of earned income, at most 47,100 kr a year; fully offset by a tax reduction of 100% of the fee

Valid from 1 January 2026

The tax reduction cannot exceed the sum of municipal income tax, state income tax, property tax and property fee. The fee is shown on the final tax notice (slutskattebesked).

Source: Skatteverket - Belopp och procent inkomstår 2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Pension-qualifying income ceilings 2026

Income ceiling for pension-qualifying income 673,038 kr (8.07 income base amounts); highest pension-qualifying income 625,500 kr (7.5 income base amounts); income base amount 83,400 kr

Valid from 1 January 2026

Ceiling for building pension rights, not a cap on employer contributions. Price base amount 2026: 59,200 kr.

Source: Skatteverket - Belopp och procent inkomstår 2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Income tax 2026 (municipal and state), on taxable earned income

Municipal income tax on all taxable income (average rate 32.38%, set by each municipality); plus 20% state income tax on taxable earned income above 643,000 kr

Valid from 1 January 2026

Taxable income means earned income after the basic deduction (grundavdrag). The state tax threshold (skiktgräns) is 643,000 kr; the brytpunkt, before the basic deduction, is 660,400 kr (760,500 kr for those aged 66 or over at the start of the year).

State tax threshold 643,000 kr; prisbasbelopp 59,200 kr

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

For 2026 the skiktgräns - the taxable income above which the 20% state income tax applies - is 643,000 kr. The brytpunkt is 660,400 kr, or 760,500 kr for those aged 66 and over. The prisbasbelopp is 59,200 kr.

For the employer

  • Confirm payroll software has loaded Skatteverket's 2026 skattetabeller before the first January pay run.
  • No separate action for the skiktgräns increase itself - withholding tables already reflect it.

For the employee

  • An employee aged 66+ has a higher brytpunkt (760,500 kr vs 660,400 kr).

In practice: Taxable income of 650,000 kr: 20% state tax on the 7,000 kr above 643,000 kr = 1,400 kr.

Source: Skatteverket - Belopp och procent inkomstår 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Standard rate holds at 31.42%, with new relief for young and older workers

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026 Effective through 2026

The standard arbetsgivaravgift stays at 31.42% for employees born 1959 or later. From 1 April 2026 to 30 September 2027, a temporary reduced rate of 20.81% applies to employees born 2003–2007, on pay up to 25,000 kr a month. Separately, from 1 January 2026, employers pay only the 10.21% old-age pension contribution - not the full rate - on pay to employees born 1938–1958.

For the employer

  • Flag employees born 2003–2007 in payroll so the reduced 20.81% rate applies correctly on their first 25,000 kr of monthly pay from April 2026.
  • Apply the 10.21% pension-only rate to employees born 1938–1958.

For the employee

  • Arbetsgivaravgift is paid entirely by the employer and does not appear on a payslip or reduce net pay - though the youth relief is designed to make employers more willing to hire younger staff.

In practice: On a 22-year-old employee (born 2004) earning 24,000 kr a month from April 2026, the employer pays 24,000 × 20.81% ≈ 4,994 kr in arbetsgivaravgift instead of the standard 24,000 × 31.42% ≈ 7,541 kr - a saving of roughly 2,547 kr a month on that one employee.

Source: Skatteverket - Arbetsgivaravgifter · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Sick pay and parental pay ceiling rises to 592,000 kr

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The maximum SGI - the income base for sjukpenning (sickness benefit) and föräldrapenning (parental benefit) - is 10 times the prisbasbelopp. With the prisbasbelopp at 59,200 kr in 2026, the maximum SGI is 592,000 kr a year.

For the employer

  • Update any internal top-up scheme that references the SGI ceiling.

For the employee

  • Sickness and parental benefits are calculated on income capped at 592,000 kr for 2026.

Source: Försäkringskassan - Aktuella belopp · Payroll impact: Low: little or no payroll configuration needed · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Växa-stöd becomes a claim-back relief

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

From 1 January 2026 the växa-stöd employer-contribution relief for a business's first hires changes from a reduced rate at source to a reimbursement model: employers pay the full arbetsgivaravgift and then request repayment retroactively from Skatteverket.

For the employer

  • If claiming växa-stöd, pay the full arbetsgivaravgift and file a reimbursement request with Skatteverket.

For the employee

  • No direct impact - this changes employer reporting mechanics, not payslip figures.

Source: Grant Thornton Sweden - Nya regler för arbetsgivare 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

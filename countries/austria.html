Minimum wage (no statutory national minimum)

Austria has no statutory minimum wage that applies to all employees; minimum wages are set mainly in sector collective agreements (Kollektivverträge), which cannot be undercut by contract. Most agreements provide at least €1,700 gross, 14 times a year

About 95% of employees are covered by a collective agreement (over 800 agreements). Page last updated 12 August 2025. Rate depends on the applicable Kollektivvertrag.

Source: Sozialministerium (BMASGPK) - Mindestlohn in Österreich · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Official table, status 1 January 2026. Housing and Chamber of Labour contributions are listed in the same table. Special rules (e.g. older employees, freelance contracts, marginal employment) are not covered.

Income tax scale (Einkommensteuertarif) 2026

Up to €13,539: 0%; over €13,539 to €21,992: 20%; to €36,458: 30%; to €70,365: 40%; to €104,859: 48%; to €1,000,000: 50%; over €1,000,000: 55% (limited until 2029, then 50%)

Valid from 1 January 2026

Tariff brackets indexed by two-thirds of inflation

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The Inflationsanpassungsverordnung 2026, published on 30 August 2025 (BGBl. II Nr. 191/2025), raises the inflation-linked income-tax thresholds and deduction amounts by 1.733% - two-thirds of the inflation rate. Income up to €13,539 is now taxed at 0% (previously €13,308), and the 50% rate now applies above €104,859 (previously €103,072).

For the employer

  • Confirm payroll software has loaded the 2026 Lohnsteuer tariff stages before the first January pay run.
  • No separate filing is needed - the indexed brackets apply automatically via the standard payroll calculation.

For the employee

  • Only two-thirds of inflation is offset by the indexation, leaving some residual cold progression on an inflation-matching pay rise.

Source: EY Austria - Inflationsanpassungsverordnung 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Höchstbeitragsgrundlage rises to €6,930 a month

Status: In force 2026

The official 2026 ASVG maximum contribution base (Höchstbeitragsgrundlage (German) - maximum contribution base) (Höchstbeitragsgrundlage) is €6,930 gross a month (€231 a day).

For the employer

  • Update the contribution ceiling in payroll software for 2026.

For the employee

  • Income above the Höchstbeitragsgrundlage is not subject to further social insurance contributions.

Source: oesterreich.gv.at - Höchstbeitragsgrundlage · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026 · Updated 3 October 2026

Gewerbe, Handwerk und Dienstleistung minimums rise 2.70%

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

Under the 2026 collective agreement for trade, craft and services (Gewerbe, Handwerk und Dienstleistung), monthly minimum basic salaries rise by 2.70% in all employment groups from 1 January 2026, and apprentice pay rises 3.0% in all four apprenticeship years. Other sectors have their own Kollektivvertrag increases.

For the employer

  • Apply the specific sector Kollektivvertrag increase relevant to the business - there is no single national minimum wage.
  • An increase in the agreed minimum automatically affects only staff paid at the old minimum; above-minimum pay depends on the agreement's own terms.

For the employee

  • Employees paid at the collectively agreed minimum in this sector receive a 2.70% increase from January 2026.

Source: WKO - Kollektivvertrag Gewerbe, Handwerk und Dienstleistung 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Working hours mandatory on social insurance registration

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

From 1 January 2026, employers must always state working hours when registering an employee with social insurance, and must report all relevant changes - such as working hours, pay or contract terms - immediately as part of their ongoing ELDA reports.

For the employer

  • Capture and submit working hours at the point of social insurance registration.
  • Build a process to report changes to hours, pay or contract terms via ELDA as they occur.

For the employee

  • Contract changes are documented more clearly in social insurance records.

Source: Remm Steuerberatung - Angabe der Arbeitszeit bei Anmeldung ab 1.1.2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

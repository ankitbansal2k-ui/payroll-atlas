Guaranteed minimum gross monthly wage (salariul de bază minim brut pe țară)

4,325 lei gross per month from 1 July 2026, for a normal working time of 166.667 hours a month on average (25.949 lei per hour)

Valid from 1 July 2026

Government Decision (HG) 146/2026 of 12 March 2026, Monitorul Oficial no. 196 of 13 March 2026. The amount excludes bonuses and other additions. It replaces HG 1,506/2024.

Employer contributions on salaries

Work insurance contribution (CAM) 2.25% of gross salary income (art. 220^3); employer social insurance (CAS) only in particular working conditions 4% or special working conditions 8% (art. 138)

Codul fiscal, consolidated text on the ANAF site (last updated by OUG 38/2026). Calculated and paid by the 25th of the month after the salary month (art. 220^6). Exemptions and special sectors not covered.

Source: ANAF - Legea nr. 227/2015 privind Codul fiscal · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employee contributions on salaries

Social insurance (CAS) 25% and health insurance (CASS) 10% of gross salary income, calculated and withheld by the employer

Codul fiscal art. 138(a), 156, 146 and 168: paid by the 25th of the month after the salary month. The monthly base is the gross salary income (art. 139, 157). Exemptions and special sectors not covered.

Source: ANAF - Legea nr. 227/2015 privind Codul fiscal · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Personal income tax on employment income

Flat 10% income tax rate on taxable salary income

Codul fiscal art. 64(1)(b): a single rate of 10% on taxable income from salaries and assimilated income. Personal deductions and the rules for other income types are not covered.

Minimum wage set at 4,325 lei from 1 July 2026

Status: In force 1 Jul 2026

Government Decision (HG) 146/2026 of 12 March 2026 sets the guaranteed gross minimum basic salary at 4,325 lei a month from 1 July 2026, for a normal average of 166.667 working hours a month (25.949 lei an hour). It repeals HG 1506/2024 from that date.

For the employer

  • Pay at least 4,325 lei gross a month (25.949 lei an hour) for the normal working programme from 1 July 2026; the amount is stated in money and excludes bonuses and other additions.

For the employee

  • Employees on the minimum wage are entitled to a gross basic salary of at least 4,325 lei a month from 1 July 2026.

Source: Guvernul Romaniei - Hotararea nr. 146/2026 (Monitorul Oficial 196/13.03.2026) · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026 · Updated 4 October 2026

New Declaration 112 form from July 2026 income

Status: In force 25 Aug 2026 Effective 25 Aug 2026 filing

ANAF Order 605/2026 introduces a new version of Declaration 112 (the combined tax and contributions return), used for July 2026 income filed by 25 August 2026. It reflects the reduction of the non-taxable minimum-wage allowance from 300 lei to 200 lei a month for July-December 2026 (under OUG 89/2025), changes to medical-leave reporting, and an updated nomenclature for categories of insured persons.

For the employer

  • Update Declaration 112 filing software to the new version before filing July 2026 income by 25 August 2026.
  • Remap payroll codes to the updated insured-person nomenclature and medical-leave fields.

For the employee

  • No direct impact beyond the smaller 200 lei non-taxable allowance for minimum-wage earners.

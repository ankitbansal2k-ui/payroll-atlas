🇨🇷 Costa Rica: payroll law changes

Private-sector minimum wages up 1.63% for 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

Decree No. 45303-MTSS sets private-sector minimum wages from 1 January 2026 with a general increase of 1.63%.

For the employer

  • General increase of 1.63% for unskilled workers.
  • Minimums range from ¢12,436.41 per day (unskilled) to ¢796,921.00 per month (university licentiate).

For the employee

  • Differentiated increases apply to domestic work and certain specialised occupations.

CCSS pension (IVM) contributions rise: employer 5.58%, employee 4.33%

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026 Scheduled step, planned to 31 Dec 2028

From 1 January 2026 the CCSS Invalidity, Old Age and Death (IVM) contributions rise under the gradual 1.00% increase every 3 years in Transitory XI of the IVM Regulation: employer 5.42% to 5.58% and employee 4.17% to 4.33%, with the state contribution at 1.75% (total IVM 11.66%). Total employer charges become 26.83% and total employee charges 10.83% (37.66% combined), with the rates planned to apply until 31 December 2028.

For the employer

  • Apply the new IVM rates from the first pay period of 2026; the other components of the employer-employee contributions are unchanged.
  • Update payroll to total employer charges of 26.83% of gross salary.

For the employee

  • Employee total social charges are 10.83% of gross salary, of which IVM is 4.33%.

Source is an EY Central America tax alert dated 15 December 2025 (professional source); the CCSS site could not be retrieved.

2026 salary tax bands: exempt up to CRC 918,000 a month

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

Executive Decree No. 45333-H (La Gaceta No. 229, 5 December 2025) sets the 2026 income tax bands for employees: 0% up to CRC 918,000 (CRC 922,000 in 2025), 10% above that up to CRC 1,347,000, 15% up to CRC 2,364,000, 20% up to CRC 4,727,000 and 25% above. The monthly tax credits are CRC 1,710 per child and CRC 2,590 for a spouse.

For the employer

  • Load the 2026 bands and credits into salary tax withholding from January 2026.
  • Apply the CRC 1,710 per-child and CRC 2,590 spouse credits where the employee claims them.

For the employee

  • The tax-free monthly salary threshold falls slightly from CRC 922,000 to CRC 918,000 because of the -0.38% CPI variation.

Source is an EY Central America tax alert dated 11 December 2025 (professional source); the Hacienda site was not retrievable. The 2026 tax period is the calendar year.

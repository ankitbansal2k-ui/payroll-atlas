Minimum wage rises to €780

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The minimum monthly wage within normal working hours rises from €740 to €780 from 1 January 2026.

For the employer

  • Raise pay for employees below €780 from the January 2026 pay run.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage workers receive €40 more a month.

In practice: €780 - €740 = €40.

Source: LV portāls - Minimālā alga 2026. gadā būs 780 eiro · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Non-taxable minimum rises to €550 a month

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The fixed monthly non-taxable minimum rises from €510 to €550 in 2026. IIN rates remain 25.5% on annual income up to €105,300 and 33% above, with an additional 3% on annual income over €200,000.

For the employer

  • Apply the €550 non-taxable minimum in monthly withholding for employees who submitted a tax booklet.

For the employee

  • Slightly lower withholding for employees.

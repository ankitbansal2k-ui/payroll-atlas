Statutory minimum hourly wage (wettelijk minimumuurloon)

Age 21 and over: €14.99 gross per hour from 1 July 2026 (€14.71 from 1 January 2026); youth rates from 1 July 2026: age 20 €11.99, 19 €8.99, 18 €7.50, 17 €5.92, 16 €5.17, 15 €4.50 per hour

Valid from 1 July 2026

The page also states there are no fixed minimum monthly, weekly or daily wages since 2024: those depend on hours worked. It lists amounts per 1 July 2026 and per 1 January 2026.

Source: Rijksoverheid.nl - Bedragen minimumloon 2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employer payroll premiums (werknemersverzekeringen and Zvw) 2026

AWf premium: low rate 2.74%, high rate 7.74%; differentiated Aof premium: low 6.27%, high 7.63%; differentiated Whk premium: see notification/decision per employer; Wko surcharge 0.50%; Ufo premium 0.68%; employer levy under the Health Insurance Act (werkgeversheffing Zvw) 6.10%

Valid from 1 January 2026

From Belastingdienst tables 9 and 12 (Uitgave 3, 3 March 2026). Premiums apply to wages up to the maximum premium wage (see ssCeiling). Which low or high AWf/Aof rate applies is set out in Belastingdienst guidance and is not detailed here.

Source: Belastingdienst - Tarieven, bedragen en percentages loonheffingen vanaf 1 januari 2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Employee national insurance premiums (volksverzekeringen) 2026

Collected through the first wage-tax bracket for employees below AOW age: AOW premium 17.90%, Anw premium 0.10%, Wlz premium 9.65% (together with 8.10% wage tax = 35.75% on income up to €38,883)

Valid from 1 January 2026

For employees at or above AOW age the first bracket is 17.85% (no AOW premium).

Source: Belastingdienst - Tarieven, bedragen en percentages loonheffingen vanaf 1 januari 2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Maximum premium wage (maximumpremieloon / maximumbijdrageloon) 2026

€79,409.00 per year; €19,852.25 per quarter; €6,617.41 per month; €6,108.38 per 4 weeks; €1,527.09 per week; €305.41 per day (same maxima for employee insurances and the Health Insurance Act levy)

Valid from 1 January 2026

Source: Belastingdienst - Tarieven, bedragen en percentages loonheffingen vanaf 1 januari 2026 · As of 4 October 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

Box 1 income tax / national insurance bands 2026 (below AOW age)

Up to €38,883: 35.75%; over €38,883 up to €78,426: 37.56%; over €78,426: 49.50%

Valid from 1 January 2026

First-bracket rate includes AOW, Anw and Wlz premiums. At AOW age (born 1946 or later) the first bracket is 17.85%; if born before 1946 it runs to €41,123 at 17.85%. Same rates in Belastingdienst loonheffingen 2026 table 1. Tax credits not covered.

Three-bracket box 1 scale and tax credits for 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

Box 1 rates for 2026 (below AOW age): 35.75% up to €38,883, 37.56% from €38,883 to €78,426, and 49.50% above €78,426. The algemene heffingskorting is up to €3,115 and the arbeidskorting peaks at €5,685 at €45,592 of income.

For the employer

  • Use the Belastingdienst 2026 wage tax tables from the first January pay run.

For the employee

  • Tax credits are applied through payroll withholding.

Source: Belastingdienst - Voorlopige aanslag 2026: gebruikte tarieven en heffingskortingen · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Minimum wage rises to €14.99 an hour from 1 July 2026

Status: In force 1 Jul 2026

The statutory minimum wage for workers aged 21 and over was €14.71 an hour from 1 January 2026 and rose to €14.99 an hour from 1 July 2026.

For the employer

  • Raise any worker aged 21+ below €14.99 an hour from the July 2026 pay run.

For the employee

  • Minimum-wage workers receive the increase automatically.

Source: Rijksoverheid - Bedragen minimumloon 2026 · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 30 September 2026

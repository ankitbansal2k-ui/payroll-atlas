Municipality-set income tax rates published for 2026

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

Income tax rates are set by each local government unit by decision, within limits in the Income Tax Act, published in Narodne novine and taking effect on 1 January of the following year. Porezna uprava published the updated table of 2026 rates, based on decisions published by 15 December, on 16 December 2025.

For the employer

  • Apply the 2026 rate for each employee's municipality of residence from the Porezna uprava table - rates vary by location.

For the employee

  • Two employees on identical salaries in different municipalities can see different net pay.

Source: Porezna uprava - Stope godišnjeg poreza na dohodak za 2026. godinu · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

Minimum wage rises to €1,050, with state covering the difference for three months

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

The Government set the gross minimum wage for 2026 at €1,050 a month, up from the current €970. Employers paying the minimum wage can receive the difference between €970 and €1,050 from the state for the first three months.

For the employer

  • Raise any employee paid below €1,050 gross from the January 2026 pay run.
  • Check eligibility for the compensation covering the €970 to €1,050 difference for the first three months.

For the employee

  • Workers on the minimum wage get an €80 monthly increase from January 2026.

In practice: €1,050 - €970 = €80 a month per minimum-wage worker.

Source: Vlada Republike Hrvatske - Minimalna bruto plaća za iduću godinu 1.050 eura · Payroll impact: Medium: new rates, thresholds or bands to load and check · Last verified 1 October 2026 · Added 8 September 2026

JOPPD form updated with new tax-exempt income codes

Status: In force 1 Jan 2026

From 1 January 2026, a new version of the JOPPD (Obrazac JOPPD - Izvješće o primicima, porezu na dohodak te doprinosima za obvezna osiguranja (Croatian) - report on receipts, income tax and mandatory insurance contributions) form applies, adding new codes for tax-exempt receipts in field 15.1. The new version of the ePorezna support software is 1.0.40.

For the employer

  • Update JOPPD-filing software to version 1.0.40 before filing 2026 reports.

For the employee

  • No direct impact - this is a technical update to employer reporting.

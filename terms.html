Information only

Payroll Atlas summarises statutory and legislative payroll changes. It is general information, not legal, tax, accounting or employment advice, and it does not create any advisory relationship. Payroll law is detailed and changes often. Do not rely on a summary here for a decision: read the linked source and take advice from a qualified professional in the relevant country.

Accuracy and completeness

  • Each entry links to the page it was checked against, and shows the date its sources were last checked. Rules may have changed since then.
  • Coverage is partial. Some countries have only one or two entries, and absence of an entry does not mean nothing has changed.
  • Entries marked Draft — not yet law describe proposals. Their content, dates and rates may change or never take effect.
  • Some sources are professional publications (for example tax advisory firms) rather than government pages, where an official page could not be retrieved. The entry shows which source it uses.

a-melding

The a-melding - monthly report to NAV, SSB and the Tax Administration (Norwegian)

Norway's monthly employer report to NAV, Statistics Norway (SSB) and the Tax Administration. It covers income, employment, withholding tax, employer's national insurance contributions and financial activity tax.

Used in: 🇳🇴 Norway · Source: Skatteetaten - The a-melding · Checked 4 October 2026

AFP

Administradora de Fondos de Pensiones (Spanish) - pension fund administrator

In Chile's mandatory individual-capitalisation pension system, the administrator where workers deposit their mandatory pension savings. The pension funds belong to the members and are separate from the administrator's own capital.

Used in: 🇨🇱 Chile · Source: Superintendencia de Pensiones (Chile) - Sistema de AFP · Checked 4 October 2026

CPF

Central Provident Fund

Singapore's Central Provident Fund. Employers pay CPF contributions for employees who are Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents, at rates that depend on citizenship status, age and wages, and may recover the employee's share from wages.

Used in: 🇸🇬 Singapore · Source: CPF Board - How much CPF contributions to pay · Checked 4 October 2026

DmfA

Déclaration multifonctionelle / multifunctionele Aangifte - multifunctional declaration

Belgium's employer declaration of each employee's wage and working-time data. It replaced the quarterly RSZ declaration from the first quarter of 2003. Sickness insurance, unemployment, pensions, occupational risks, child allowances and annual leave bodies use the same data.

Used in: 🇧🇪 Belgium · Source: socialsecurity.be - DmfA: algemeen · Checked 4 October 2026

EPF

Employees' Provident Fund

Malaysia's compulsory retirement savings scheme for employees working in Malaysia. It covers mandatory contributions by all employees, including Malaysian citizens, permanent residents and non-Malaysian citizens. Rates depend on citizenship status, age and monthly wages.

Used in: 🇲🇾 Malaysia · Source: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries - Malaysia: Other taxes · Checked 4 October 2026

GMMI

Gewaarborgd gemiddeld minimummaandinkomen (GGMMI, Dutch) - guaranteed average minimum monthly income

Also written: GGMMI

Belgium's guaranteed average minimum monthly income, set by collective labour agreement no. 43. It covers employees aged 18 or over with an employment contract; no wage may be lower. Agreement no. 50 covers students aged 18 to 20.

Used in: 🇧🇪 Belgium · Source: Nationale Arbeidsraad (CNT-NAR) - Minimumloon · Checked 4 October 2026

GOSI

General Organization for Social Insurance

Saudi Arabia's social insurance organisation. It applies the Social Insurance Law, follows up compulsory insurance coverage, collects contributions from employers and pays benefits to eligible contributors or their families.

Used in: 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia · Source: GOSI - Brief on the General Organization for Social Insurance · Checked 4 October 2026

Höchstbeitragsgrundlage

Höchstbeitragsgrundlage (German) - maximum contribution base

Also written: maximum contribution base

Austria's income threshold up to which social insurance contributions must be paid. Contributions are due only on the part of income below it, set as a monthly and daily amount.

Used in: 🇦🇹 Austria · Source: oesterreich.gv.at - Höchstbeitragsgrundlage · Checked 4 October 2026

INSS

Instituto Nacional do Seguro Social (Portuguese) - National Social Security Institute

Brazil's national social security institute. Its monthly contribution table sets progressive contribution rates up to a maximum contribution base for employees, domestic employees and casual workers (trabalhadores avulsos).

Used in: 🇧🇷 Brazil · Source: INSS (gov.br) - Tabela de contribuição mensal · Checked 4 October 2026

IRPF

Imposto sobre a Renda das Pessoas Físicas (Portuguese) - individual income tax

Brazil's personal income tax. The Receita Federal publishes a progressive monthly table, with a fixed monthly deduction per dependant and, from January 2026, a reduction that brings tax to zero for lower monthly incomes.

Used in: 🇧🇷 Brazil · Source: Receita Federal - Tabelas 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

JMHZ

Jednotné měsíční hlášení zaměstnavatele (Czech) - unified (single) monthly employer report

Czechia's unified electronic monthly employer report, in force from 1 January 2026 and phased in through July 2026. It lets employers meet reporting and record-keeping duties to several state bodies, such as ČSSZ, in one submission.

Used in: 🇨🇿 Czech Republic · Source: ČSSZ - Co je JMHZ · Checked 4 October 2026

JOPPD

Obrazac JOPPD - Izvješće o primicima, porezu na dohodak te doprinosima za obvezna osiguranja (Croatian) - report on receipts, income tax and mandatory insurance contributions

Also written: Obrazac JOPPD

Croatia's Tax Administration form for reporting receipts, income tax and mandatory insurance contributions. It has been in use since 1 January 2014.

Used in: 🇭🇷 Croatia · Source: Porezna uprava - Obrazac JOPPD · Checked 4 October 2026

KiwiSaver

KiwiSaver - New Zealand's voluntary, work-based retirement savings scheme

New Zealand's voluntary, work-based retirement savings scheme. Employers deduct employee contributions from pay and must also make a compulsory minimum employer contribution, unless they already contribute to another superannuation fund.

Used in: 🇳🇿 New Zealand · Source: Inland Revenue - How KiwiSaver works · Checked 4 October 2026

MMA

Minimalioji mėnesinė alga (Lithuanian) - minimum monthly wage

Lithuania's minimum monthly wage. The Government sets the amount for each year by resolution, together with a minimum hourly rate.

Used in: 🇱🇹 Lithuania · Source: VMI - Gyventojų pajamų mokesčio pakeitimai nuo 2026 m. · Checked 4 October 2026

MPF

Mandatory Provident Fund

Hong Kong's Mandatory Provident Fund system, launched in December 2000 under the Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes Ordinance. It is the second pillar of Hong Kong's retirement protection framework.

Used in: 🇭🇰 Hong Kong · Source: MPFA - MPF System · Checked 4 October 2026

PAYE

Pay As You Earn

The UK system HMRC uses to collect Income Tax and National Insurance from employment. Employers deduct them from employees' pay and report the payments and deductions to HMRC on or before each payday.

Used in: 🇬🇧 United Kingdom · Source: GOV.UK - PAYE and payroll for employers · Checked 4 October 2026

PMSS

Monthly value of the plafond de la Sécurité sociale (French social security ceiling)

Also written: PASS

The monthly value of France's social security ceiling (plafond de la Sécurité sociale). The annual value is the PASS. The ceiling is used to work out social contributions on salary, exemption thresholds for severance pay and intern pay.

Used in: 🇫🇷 France · Source: Service-Public.fr - Plafond de la sécurité sociale 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

PPh 21

Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 21 (Indonesian) - Article 21 income tax

Indonesia's income tax withheld on income from work, services or activities, including salaries and wages. Employers withhold it from employees' pay.

Used in: 🇮🇩 Indonesia · Source: Direktorat Jenderal Pajak - PPh Pasal 21/26 · Checked 4 October 2026

PRSI

Pay Related Social Insurance

Ireland's social insurance contribution, paid by employers and employees. The rate depends on the employee's pay and PRSI class. It is the main source of funding for social welfare payments and is passed to the Department of Social Protection.

Used in: 🇮🇪 Ireland · Source: Revenue - Pay Related Social Insurance (PRSI) · Checked 4 October 2026

RMV

Remuneración Mínima Vital (Spanish) - minimum wage

Peru's minimum wage for workers under the private-sector labour regime. It is changed by supreme decree, here following consensus reached in the Consejo Nacional de Trabajo y Promoción del Empleo.

Used in: 🇵🇪 Peru · Source: El Peruano - Decreto Supremo Nº 015-2026-TR · Checked 4 October 2026

RSZ

RSZ (Dutch) / ONSS (French) - Belgium's national social security office

Also written: ONSS

Belgium's national social security office. Since 1945 it has collected social contributions, gathered and checked employers' wage and working-time data, supported employers with their declarations and contributed to financing the social security system.

Used in: 🇧🇪 Belgium · Source: RSZ - Wie zijn wij? (Identiteit) · Checked 4 October 2026

SMIC

Salaire minimum interprofessionnel de croissance (French) - minimum wage

Also written: Smic

France's minimum wage. It is revalued every 1 January and is also raised automatically during the year when consumer prices rise by at least 2% from the level at the last revaluation.

Used in: 🇫🇷 France · Source: Service-Public.fr - Smic revaluation (June 2026) · Checked 4 October 2026

SMVM

Salario Mínimo, Vital y Móvil (Spanish) - minimum, living and adjustable wage

Argentina's national minimum wage, set by the Consejo Nacional del Empleo, la Productividad y el Salario Mínimo, Vital y Móvil. It covers workers under Ley 20.744, the agricultural regime and the national public administration, with monthly and hourly amounts.

Used in: 🇦🇷 Argentina · Source: Argentina.gob.ar - Resolución 4/2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

SSP

Statutory Sick Pay

The UK minimum sick pay that employers must pay to eligible employees who are too ill to work, for up to 28 weeks. Employers may pay more under their own sick pay scheme, but not less.

Used in: 🇬🇧 United Kingdom · Source: GOV.UK - Statutory Sick Pay (SSP): Overview · Checked 4 October 2026

SSS

Social Security System

The Philippines' Social Security System, which collects mandatory contributions under the Social Security Act of 2018 (Republic Act No. 11199). Employer and employee each pay a share of a Monthly Salary Credit (MSC) with a minimum and a maximum.

Used in: 🇵🇭 Philippines · Source: Forvis Mazars Philippines - SSS contribution rates increased to 15% · Checked 4 October 2026

UIT

Unidad Impositiva Tributaria (Spanish) - tax reference unit

Peru's tax reference unit: a value set each year by supreme decree of the Ministry of Economy and Finance and used as a reference index in tax rules, so thresholds and limits can be expressed as multiples of it.

Used in: 🇵🇪 Peru · Source: El Peruano - Decreto Supremo que aprueba el valor de la UIT durante el año 2026 · Checked 4 October 2026

USC

Universal Social Charge

An Irish tax on total income, including employment income. Once total income exceeds the set threshold, USC is charged on the full income at the standard or reduced rate, depending on circumstances. Some types of income are exempt.

Used in: 🇮🇪 Ireland · Source: Revenue - Universal Social Charge (USC) · Checked 4 October 2026

VDU

Vidutinis darbo užmokestis (Lithuanian) - average wage

Lithuania's average wage figure, used to set the base for state social insurance contributions for the year. Personal income tax bands are also expressed as multiples of it, such as 36 VDU and 60 VDU.

Used in: 🇱🇹 Lithuania · Source: VMI - Apie gyventojų pajamų mokesčio tarifus · Checked 4 October 2026

WPS (Bahrain)

Wages Protection System

Also written: WPS

Bahrain's system that lets employers pay employee wages through banks and financial institutions approved by the Central Bank of Bahrain. The Labour Market Regulatory Authority (LMRA) has launched an enhanced version.

Used in: 🇧🇭 Bahrain · Source: LMRA (Bahrain) - LMRA Launches the Enhanced Wages Protection System · Checked 4 October 2026

WPS (Qatar)

Wage Protection System (also written Wages Protection System)

Also written: WPS

Qatar's centralized system, launched in 2010 as an initiative between Qatar Central Bank and the Ministry of Labour, to manage and verify wage payments from employers to employees under law no. 14 of 2004.

Used in: 🇶🇦 Qatar · Source: Qatar Central Bank - Retail payment systems · Checked 4 October 2026

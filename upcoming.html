Keep your calendar up to date

Subscribing means your calendar checks this page for new and changed dates by itself after each fortnightly update. A downloaded file is a one-off copy that never changes.

  • Google Calendar: Other calendars, then +, then From URL, and paste https://www.intelligentpayroll.eu/calendar.ics
  • Outlook: Add calendar, then Subscribe from web, and paste the same address.
  • Apple Calendar: File, then New Calendar Subscription, and paste the same address.

Calendars refresh on their own schedule (Google can take a day). Changes with only a year are not included, because they have no date to put in a calendar. Calendars for single countries are on each country page, at the same kind of address (for example https://www.intelligentpayroll.eu/countries/finland.ics).
